Congratulations wishes

Good news deserves a quick, glad reply. Pick the occasion and send it while the news is still fresh.

New job and promotion

  • Congratulations on the new job! They are lucky to have you. Go show them why.

  • Congratulations on your promotion! Nobody who has watched you work is surprised.

  • New role, bigger desk, same good person. Congratulations!

  • Heard the news. So well deserved. Congratulations, and the treat is due.

  • Congratulations on your first job! The first salary has a special taste. Enjoy it.

Studies

  • Congratulations on your result! All that work, and now this. Proud of you.

  • Congratulations, graduate! The cap fits. The world is waiting.

  • Doctor at last! Congratulations on finishing the long road. We always knew.

  • Congratulations on clearing the exam. You did the hard part: you kept going.

Business, vehicle and awards

  • Congratulations on your new shop! May the first customer come early and the last one leave happy.

  • Best wishes for your new business. May it grow steadily, like everything built with honest work.

  • Congratulations on the new car! Drive safe, and remember who asked for the first ride.

  • Congratulations on the new bike! Helmet on, always.

  • Congratulations on the award. It is good to see good work noticed.

  • नई नौकरी की ढेर सारी शुभकामनाएँ। आगे और तरक्की करें।

  • प्रमोशन की बधाई! अब मिठाई कब खिला रहे हैं?

  • नए कारोबार की हार्दिक शुभकामनाएँ। बरकत बनी रहे।

  • नई गाड़ी मुबारक हो! सँभलकर चलाइए और पहली सवारी हमें कराइए।

  • सफलता की बधाई। यह तो बस शुरुआत है।

  • ਨਵੀਂ ਨੌਕਰੀ ਦੀਆਂ ਮੁਬਾਰਕਾਂ। ਵਾਹਿਗੁਰੂ ਹੋਰ ਤਰੱਕੀਆਂ ਬਖ਼ਸ਼ੇ।

Make it yours

  • Reply the same day. A late congratulation feels like a duty.
  • Say what you saw them do to earn it. "You worked for this" matters to people.
