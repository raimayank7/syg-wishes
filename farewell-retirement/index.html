Farewell and retirement wishes

A farewell wish should thank the person and send them forward. These lines fit a card signed by the team, a message on the last day or a few words at the send-off.

For a colleague who is leaving

  • Wishing you all the best in your new role. The office will be quieter, and a little less fun.

  • Farewell and good luck! Thank you for the help, the patience and the tea breaks.

  • You leave the team better than you found it. All the best for what comes next.

  • Goodbye is not the right word. Stay in touch. Best wishes for the new job.

  • We will miss your work and your jokes, in that order on most days. Good luck!

For a boss or teacher

  • Thank you for your guidance, Sir. Much of what we do right, we learnt from you. Best wishes for the future.

  • Ma'am, you led with fairness and we worked without fear. Thank you, and all the best.

  • Farewell to a teacher who made the school a better place. We will carry your lessons with us.

Retirement

  • Happy retirement! You gave the work your best years. Now give the best years to yourself.

  • Congratulations on your retirement. No alarms, no meetings, only your own timetable. Enjoy every day.

  • Wishing you a healthy and happy retired life. May your mornings be slow and your evenings be full.

  • After years of service, the rest is well earned. Happy retirement, and thank you.
