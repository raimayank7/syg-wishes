Raksha Bandhan wishes

Rakhi is the one day siblings say out loud what they tease each other about all year. Choose a wish from a sister, from a brother or for a sibling who is far away.

From a sister

  • Happy Raksha Bandhan, bhai. The thread is small. What it stands for is not. Thank you for always being there.

  • Happy Rakhi to my brother, who still thinks he is the boss. Love you anyway.

  • To my brother: you were my first friend and my first fight. Happy Raksha Bandhan.

  • Happy Raksha Bandhan, bhaiya. No gift needed. Just keep answering my calls.

From a brother

  • Happy Raksha Bandhan, sis. My promise is the same as every year: I am one call away.

  • Happy Rakhi to my sister, who knows all my secrets and has not used them yet. Thank you.

  • Didi, you tied a thread on my wrist and held the whole family together. Happy Raksha Bandhan.

  • Happy Raksha Bandhan, chhoti. Whatever you need, whenever you need it. That is the deal.

For a sibling far away

  • The rakhi is in the post and my heart came with it. Happy Raksha Bandhan from far away.

  • Different cities, same childhood. Happy Rakhi. Missing you a little extra today.

  • I could not come this year, but my promise travels. Happy Raksha Bandhan.

हिन्दी में

  • रक्षाबंधन की हार्दिक शुभकामनाएँ, भैया। यह धागा नहीं, मेरी दुआ है जो हमेशा आपके साथ रहेगी।

  • मेरी प्यारी बहन को राखी की बधाई। तेरी खुशी मेरी ज़िम्मेदारी है।

  • भाई, राखी मुबारक! इस बार गिफ्ट अच्छा देना, पिछली बार का हिसाब बाकी है।

  • दूर हूँ, पर दिल से आज भी तेरी कलाई पर राखी बाँध रही हूँ। रक्षाबंधन मुबारक।

  • बचपन की लड़ाइयाँ याद आती हैं तो हँसी आ जाती है। राखी की ढेर सारी शुभकामनाएँ, दीदी।
