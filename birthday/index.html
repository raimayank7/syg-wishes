Birthday wishes

A birthday wish works when it sounds like it was written for one person. Pick the group that fits, copy a wish, then add one detail only the two of you know: a nickname, a place, a small memory.

Every wish here is written by us and is free to send. Use the filter to see wishes for one person, or only Hindi or Punjabi.

The formula for a birthday wish people keep

Name, wish, one true detail, a look ahead. "Happy birthday, Ritu. May this year be kinder than the last. I still think of the day you stood outside my exam hall for three hours. Dinner on me this Saturday." Four short sentences, and no forward can compete with it. The detail is the part that matters. Everything else on this page is a starting point for it.

Wishes by relationship

  • Parents and grandparents: thanks first, then the wish. Respectful words in the language they pray in.
  • Husband or wife: something you noticed this year that you never said aloud.
  • Children: what you are proud of, and it should not be marks.
  • Friends: a shared memory, or a joke that is kind underneath.
  • Boss, teacher, client: short, warm and formal. No jokes about age.

Late, forgotten or far away

A late wish is far better than none. Say sorry in half a line and wish fully, without excuses. If the person is abroad, send the wish at their morning, not yours. If you have drifted apart, a birthday is the easiest day of the year to say hello again: nobody asks why you wrote.

What to avoid

  • Jokes about age, weight or being unmarried.
  • Wishing in a group and nowhere else, if the person is close to you.
  • A bare "HBD". If the birthday is worth a message, it is worth two real words.

For a verse to write in a card, see our birthday and occasion poems.

Make it yours

  • Start with the name. "Happy birthday, Ritu" reads warmer than "Happy birthday" alone.
  • Add one true memory in a single line. It turns a copied wish into your own.
  • Send it early in the day. A wish at 8 in the morning feels more thought of than one at 11 at night.
  • For elders, add a line of thanks. For children, add what you are proud of.

Questions

Can I send these wishes on WhatsApp and Instagram?

Yes. Every wish is free to copy and send to people you know, on any app, in a card or in a speech.

What do I write for a late birthday wish?

Say sorry in one short line, then wish properly. Do not explain much. See the Belated group on this page.
