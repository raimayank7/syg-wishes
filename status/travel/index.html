When to post travel lines

After you are back, or at least after you have left the place in the photo. A status that says the whole family is away for ten days also tells strangers that the house is empty. The line reads just as well a day later.

Make the place do the work

The strongest travel captions name something only that place has. The fog at Mall Road, the langar at Amritsar, the ferry at Kochi, the bus conductor's whistle in the hills. "Mountains are calling" could be anywhere. "Maggi at 9,000 feet" is a memory. If you travelled for work or to visit family, say so: a line about a night train home is travel too.

What to leave out of the photo

  • Boarding passes, tickets and luggage tags. The codes on them can be read.
  • Hotel room numbers and key cards.
  • Your live location while you are still there.
  • Other travellers who did not ask to be in your status.

Make it yours

  • Add the distance or the hours: "14 hours by bus, worth every pothole."
  • Add who came: "First trip with Papa after his retirement."

Before you post

  • Name the place. "Kasol, 6 am" under a photo is a complete status.
  • Post after you return if your home is empty while you travel.
