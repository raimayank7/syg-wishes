Love lines that stay sweet in public

A love status is read by your partner, and also by your aunt, your manager and your old classmates. The lines that survive that audience are the small, everyday ones: who makes the tea, who remembers the charger, who waits at the station. They say more than grand declarations and embarrass nobody.

For an anniversary or a birthday, a status works well next to a private message, not in place of one. Write the long version to the person. Post the short one for everyone else.

Make it yours

  • Swap in the real habit: "You steal my blanket and I still stayed."
  • Use the name you actually call them, if they are happy to have it public.
  • Add the number: "Year six. Still saving you the last bite."

What to avoid

  • Posting about someone who has not agreed to be posted about.
  • Lines about an ex, even kind ones.
  • Private jokes or details that belong in a chat.
  • Using the status to settle a quarrel in front of an audience.

Before you post

  • A line plus a photo of the two of you needs no caption.
