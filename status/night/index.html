Night and late night status

The day is loud and the night tells the truth. These lines are for the terrace, the last cup, the ceiling fan and the hour when the phone should already be down.

English

  • The day is done. I did what I could.

  • Terrace, moon, and nobody asking for anything.

  • I will sleep early tonight. Tonight's joke, done.

  • Night is when the house finally exhales.

  • My thoughts work the night shift.

  • Last tea of the day. Everything else can wait.

  • The city is quiet and so, at last, am I.

  • Phone on charge. Me too.

  • Whatever today was, it is over. Good.

  • Stars do not hurry. Good example.

  • 3 am, and the fan knows all my plans.

  • Night shift: the world sleeps and we keep it running.

  • Counting blessings. Fewer sheep required.

Two kinds of night status

One kind is calm: the moon, the quiet street, thanks for the day. The other is funny: the plan to sleep early that failed again. Both are fine. What rarely ages well is the third kind, the heavy line written at 1 am about a person or a hurt. If a line feels urgent after midnight, save it and read it at breakfast. If it still seems right, post it then.

Make it yours

  • Describe what you can hear: the fan, a distant train, a neighbour's television.
  • Name where you are: terrace, hostel room, night shift desk, the last bus.
  • End the day with one thing that went right.

For night shift workers

Nurses, guards, drivers, call centre staff and bakers see a side of the city others sleep through. A line from the night shift is real in a way no moon quote can match. "3 am, ward quiet, tea strong" needs nothing added.

A word on sleep

A night status is pleasant. A night of scrolling is not. If you notice that you are awake only because of the phone, post the line and put the phone to charge across the room.

Before you post

  • A good night line is also a kind way to tell friends you are going offline.
