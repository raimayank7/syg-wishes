Work lines without trouble

Colleagues, clients and often your manager can see your status. A light line about Monday, tea breaks or month-end is fine and even friendly. A line about a bad boss, a late salary or a named customer can be screenshotted and shown around in a minute. Keep the jokes about the day of the week, not about people.

Good moments to post

  • Monday morning, with humour.
  • After a target, a launch or a long shift, with thanks to the team.
  • On your first day, last day, or work anniversary.
  • Friday evening. Everyone agrees with a Friday line.

Make it yours

Add your trade. A teacher's Monday, a shopkeeper's Monday and a nurse's Monday are three different lines. "Shutter up at 9. Chai at 9.05." is a better work status than any quotation about success. If you run a business from your phone, remember that customers read your status too: a cheerful line about opening hours does more for you than a tired one.

What to avoid

  • Complaints about a named company, customer or colleague.
  • Photos of your screen, ID card, documents or the office whiteboard.
  • "Looking for a change" hints while you are still employed there.

Before you post

  • Keep work humour general. Do not post about a boss, a client or a colleague that anyone could recognise.
