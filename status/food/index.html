Food lines people actually like

A food status works when it makes the reader hungry or homesick. The dish matters less than the feeling around it: the first aam of summer, the wedding plate you went back for twice, the Maggi at midnight in a hostel, the thali your mother sends you off with. Home food beats restaurant food in a status almost every time.

Make it yours

  • Name the dish and the cook: "Chachi's kadhi. No recipe survives outside her kitchen."
  • Name the place: the stall, the lane, the town.
  • Confess something: "Ordered salad. Ate everyone's fries."

Photo tips for food

  • Daylight near a window is kinder than a tube light.
  • Take the photo before the first bite, and quickly. Hot food and waiting family should not both suffer.
  • A hand reaching for the plate makes the photo feel alive.

What to avoid

  • Jokes about someone's weight or appetite, including your own every single day.
  • Lines that mock what other people eat. Food habits are personal and often tied to faith.
  • Posting feast photos during a fast or a mourning period in the family.

Before you post

  • Tag the cook. Especially if the cook is your mother.
