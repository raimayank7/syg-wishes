Selfie and photo captions

A caption should add something the photo does not say. These are short, confident and never ask for compliments.

English

  • Proof that I left the house.

  • Good lighting did most of the work.

  • Me, before the tea wore off.

  • Took forty. Posting this one.

  • Not a model. Just a good angle.

  • Wearing my favourite outfit: confidence. And this kurta.

  • Hair did what it wanted. I agreed.

  • Smile first. Reasons later.

  • New haircut, same nonsense.

  • Caught in a good mood.

  • No filter. Only sunlight and sleep.

  • The camera likes me today. We will see about tomorrow.

  • Dressed up with somewhere to go, for once.

  • Sunday face.

  • Smiling because the photo took only twelve tries.

  • New spectacles. The world has edges again.

  • Dressed up, nowhere to go, posting anyway.

  • The mirror and I had a good meeting.

  • Camera shy, clearly recovering.

  • Same face, better mood.

A caption's one job

The photo already shows your face. The caption should add what the photo cannot: where you were, what just happened, or a small joke at your own expense. A caption that only says "me" in different words wastes the space.

Match the caption to the photo

  • New haircut or new look: name it lightly. "Lost some hair, found some courage."
  • Dressed up: say the occasion. "Cousin's sangeet. I was told to wear colour."
  • Plain everyday selfie: say the mood or the day. "Tuesday, surprisingly fine."
  • Mirror selfie: a joke helps. Everyone can see the phone.

Make it yours

Replace the general word with the exact one. Not "good day" but "result day". Not "new me" but "first day with spectacles". If the photo has a story, such as the bike you saved for or the saree that was your mother's, one line of that story is the best caption you will ever write.

What to avoid

  • Fishing for praise with "ugly photo, sorry".
  • Heavy filters with a caption about being natural.
  • Long quotations under a selfie. The two rarely have anything to do with each other.
  • Anything that shows your address, number plate or ID in the background.

Before you post

  • Shorter is better. If the caption is longer than two lines, cut it.
  • Funny captions work best on your least serious photo.
