Chai and rain status

Rain on the window and a cup in the hand: India's favourite status photo. These are the lines to go under it.

English

  • Rain outside, chai inside, nothing on the calendar.

  • First rain, first sip, last worry.

  • The weather has ordered pakoras on my behalf.

  • Tea is a hug in a steel glass.

  • Clouds have gathered. So have we, around the kettle.

  • Monsoon rule: one more cup.

  • Some people check the weather. I check the milk.

  • The smell of wet earth should be sold in bottles.

  • Chai does not solve problems. It makes them wait politely.

  • Window seat, light rain, strong tea. Do not disturb.

  • Evening without tea is just late afternoon.

  • Rain makes everyone a poet for ten minutes.

  • Kadak chai, soft rain, no plans.

  • Pakoras were made. Peace was restored.

  • The rain cancelled my plans and I said thank you.

  • Tea is my answer. I forgot the question.

  • Window seat, wet road, warm glass.

The right moment

The first shower of the season, a power cut with candles, a balcony at five in the evening, a roadside stall on a highway. Chai and rain lines are among the few that nearly everyone in your contacts will agree with, so they are safe on any day and need no explanation.

Put your own cup in it

Everyone has a way of taking tea, and that is where your line is. Kadak, with ginger, with elaichi, in a glass, in a kulhad, with rusk, with pakoras, with too much sugar because Nani made it. Coffee people can swap the word and keep the mood. Name the place as well: "Rain on a tin roof in Shimla" does more than "rainy vibes".

Pairing with a photo

  • Shoot the cup from above with something beside it: a book, spectacles, a window with drops.
  • Steam shows best against a dark background.
  • Keep the caption shorter than the steam lasts.

A small caution

Heavy rain is not cosy for everyone. On days when your city is flooded or people are stranded, hold the cheerful rain line for another evening.

Before you post

  • Take the photo before the tea goes cold. Then drink it. Then post.
