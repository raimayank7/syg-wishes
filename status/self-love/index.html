Self love and self respect status

Self respect is quieter than attitude. These lines are about looking after yourself: resting, saying no, and being on your own side without being against anyone.

English

  • I am learning to speak to myself the way I speak to a friend.

  • Rest is not a reward. It is part of the work.

  • I left the table where respect was not being served.

  • I said no, and the sky stayed where it was.

  • Taking my time. It is mine, after all.

  • I forgave myself for what I did not know then.

  • Not everyone has to like me. I have to live with me.

  • I watered my plants and myself today.

  • Choosing peace is not giving up. It is growing up.

  • I am my own long-term project.

  • Today I did less, and I was enough.

  • My boundaries are doors, not walls. I decide who walks in.

  • I clapped for myself today. Quietly, but still.

  • Being kind to myself is not selfish. It is where kindness starts.

Self love is not showing off

The lines people call "self love" fall into two kinds. One kind says "I am better than you". The other says "I am learning to be kind to myself". Only the second is worth posting, and it is the only kind on this page. It suits the days after a hard decision: leaving a job that was wearing you down, ending a habit, taking a break, starting again.

When to post one

  • After you have done the kind thing for yourself, not while you are still arguing about it.
  • On a slow Sunday, with a photo of something ordinary: a plant, a book, a clean desk.
  • On your birthday, in place of asking the world for anything.

Make it yours

Name the small act. "Slept eight hours. Calling it self respect." "Ate lunch away from the laptop. Growth." Small, true and slightly funny reads better than a grand statement about your worth, and it gives a tired friend permission to do the same.

What to avoid

  • Lines that put others down to lift you up.
  • Using "self love" to announce a fight or a break-up.
  • Promises about mental health. A status can share a habit. It cannot treat a problem. If you are struggling, talking to a person you trust or to a doctor is the real self care.

Before you post

  • If the line has the word "they" in it, it is probably about someone else. Choose one with "I".

Questions

What is the difference between attitude and self respect lines?

Attitude lines show confidence to others. Self respect lines are about how you treat yourself. For the first kind, see the attitude page.
