Bio lines for WhatsApp and Instagram

A bio has one job: tell a stranger who you are in one breath. Pick a line that is true about you, then swap in your own city, work or favourite thing.

  • Here for a good time and a strong cup of tea.

  • Small town heart. Big plans.

  • Learning something new, slowly.

  • Family first. Food second. Everything else later.

  • Dog person. The dog disagrees on some days.

  • Collecting sunsets, not likes.

  • Work in progress. Thank you for your patience.

  • Reads more than replies. Sorry in advance.

  • Simple person. Complicated playlist.

  • Making ordinary days count.

  • Reader of books, loser of bookmarks.

  • From a small town, with big plans.

  • Evenings are for family. Messages can wait.

  • Still learning. Ask me again next year.

  • Here for the people, not the numbers.

Funny

  • I walk into rooms and forget why. Regularly.

  • Fluent in three languages and one sarcasm.

  • Running on tea and unread messages.

  • My hobbies include eating and planning to exercise.

  • Battery low. Standards high.

  • Available for free advice nobody asked for.

Work and study

  • Accountant by day. Home cook by night.

  • Teacher. Believer in second attempts.

  • Engineer. I fix things, and sometimes people's Wi-Fi.

  • Student of commerce, and of life.

  • Building a small business with big care.

  • Doctor in the making. Please be patient with the patient.

  • Aspirant. One more attempt, one more cup of tea.

  • Teacher by day. Student always.

  • Running a small shop with a big heart.

  • Engineer. I fix things, mostly on the second try.

  • Nurse. Long shifts, longer patience.

  • Final year. Send good wishes and notes.

A simple way to write your bio

Write three plain facts about yourself on paper: what you do, where you are from, and one small thing you love. Then cut every extra word. "I am working as a nurse in Ludhiana and I love old songs" becomes "Nurse. Ludhiana. Old songs on repeat." That is a complete bio, and nobody else has it.

If you use WhatsApp for work, keep the About line useful: your shop timing, your trade, or "Replies after 6 pm". Customers read it before they message you. For a personal Instagram profile you can be lighter, but a stranger should still learn one true thing about you.

Bio for different people

  • Students: your course or class and one interest. "B.Com second year. Cricket on Sundays."
  • Working people: your work in two words, then something human. "Accountant. Weekend cook."
  • Shops and small businesses: what you sell, your town, your hours. Leave jokes for your personal profile.
  • Parents and elders: family first reads warmly. "Dadi to four. Tea at five."

What to keep out of a bio

A bio is public even when your photos are not. Leave out your full date of birth, your home address, your phone number on public profiles, and your child's school. Skip long strings of symbols and emoji too: they are hard to read aloud for people who use screen readers, and they push the real words off the screen.

Before you post

  • A true detail beats a clever line. "Teacher. Tea first." says more than a quotation.
  • Keep it under two short lines so that it is not cut off on a phone.
  • Do not put your phone number, home address or date of birth in a public bio.
