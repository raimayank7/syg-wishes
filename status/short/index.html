Why two or three words can be enough

A very short status is read fully by everyone who sees it, which is more than can be said of a paragraph. It works like a signboard: it tells people your state today and lets them fill in the rest. "Exam mode on" tells friends why you are not replying. "Home, finally" tells family you have reached.

Short lines also suit the About line under your name, where only a few words show in a chat list.

How to make your own

  • Start from a full sentence about today, then delete words until it breaks. Keep the last version that still makes sense. "I am finally back home after the trip" becomes "Home, finally."
  • Use a full stop after each word or pair for a steady beat: "Tired. Happy. Fed."
  • Name a real thing. "Tea first" is better than "Priorities".
  • In Hindi and Punjabi, drop the verb at the end. "घर वापसी" says it all.

When short is too short

One vague word such as "Hmm" or "Done" makes people worry or guess. If the line could be read as sad or angry and you are neither, add one more word to make the mood clear.

Before you post

  • Very short lines work best with a photo that explains the rest.
