Sunday and weekend status

Saturday plans, Sunday naps and the small sadness of Sunday evening. Lines for the two days that carry the other five.

English

  • Sunday rule: nothing before tea.

  • Saturday me made plans. Sunday me cancelled them.

  • Today's schedule: lunch, nap, repeat.

  • The weekend arrived. I am accepting no meetings.

  • Sunday evening: that small, familiar sadness.

  • Pyjamas till noon. Do not judge.

  • Two days are not enough. I would like to speak to a manager.

  • Family lunch, long table, longer stories.

  • Friday night: the whole weekend still in the bank.

  • Slept in the afternoon. Woke up in a new year.

  • Sunday cricket in the lane. Rules change every over.

  • My day off is Monday, and it is glorious.

  • A slow morning is the best plan I made all week.

The weekend, hour by hour

Weekend lines land best when they match the hour. Saturday morning is hope and plans. Sunday morning is late tea and newspapers. Sunday afternoon is lunch and a nap you did not plan. Sunday after six is the one mood the whole country shares. Pick the line for the hour you are actually in.

Make it yours

  • Name the Sunday dish at your house: rajma chawal, chole bhature, fish curry, puri.
  • Name the plan you cancelled and what replaced it.
  • Add the match, the market or the relative who dropped in.

Not everyone has Sunday off

Shopkeepers, nurses, drivers, police and many others work through the weekend, and for some the day off is Monday or Friday. If that is you, there is a line here for your day too. Change the day's name and the line is yours.

What to avoid

  • Party photos of friends who would rather not be seen in them on Monday morning.
  • Complaining every Sunday evening about your job where your manager can read it.

Before you post

  • A Sunday line with a photo of a plate does better than either alone.
