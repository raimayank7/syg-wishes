Good moments for a happy status

The best happy status is small and exact. A result that came, a parent's visit, the first rain, a salary day, a child's first word, a plate of something you missed. Big announcements belong in a message to the people concerned. The status is for the feeling afterwards.

Say thanks without showing off

There is a thin line between sharing joy and displaying it. Lines that thank someone, such as a teacher, a parent, a team or simply the day, stay on the right side. Lines that list what you bought, where you flew and what it cost drift to the other. If your good news may be someone else's sore spot, as with exam ranks or job offers among friends who are still waiting, keep the line gentle and short.

Make it yours

  • Name the reason: "Happy, and the reason weighs 3 kg and has my nose."
  • Name the person: "Thankful for Ritu, who made me apply."
  • Name the dish, the place or the song that did it.

What to avoid

  • "Blessed" under every photo. Used daily, the word stops meaning anything.
  • Thanking in public a person you have not thanked in private.

Before you post

  • Say what made the day good. "First salary. Bought Maa a saree." beats any general line.
