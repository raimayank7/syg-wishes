Study and exam status

For students in exam mode, aspirants on attempt number "do not ask", and anyone who has opened a book at midnight.

English

  • Exam mode: on. Social life: paused.

  • Syllabus: huge. Time: tiny. Confidence: surprisingly okay.

  • I am not ignoring you. I am ignoring everything.

  • One more chapter. I have said this four times.

  • Dear brain, please cooperate. Kind regards.

  • Studying so hard that even my tea is tired.

  • Result day: please be gentle.

  • Paper done. Whatever happened in there stays in there.

  • Hostel life: where Maggi is a food group.

  • Aspirant life: same desk, new attempt, stronger tea.

  • Books open. Phone face down. Mostly.

  • Last exam done. Sleep, I am coming home.

  • Syllabus: large. Time: small. Tea: ready.

  • Studying. If I reply, scold me.

  • One more chapter, then sleep. I said that an hour ago.

  • My notes are colour coded. My mind is not.

  • Last paper tomorrow. Freedom is loading.

What a study status is for

It tells friends why you have gone quiet, and it keeps you honest. "Exam mode on, back on the 14th" stops a hundred "where are you?" messages. Some students post a daily count, such as "Day 9: organic chemistry", and find that the small audience keeps them at the desk.

Make it yours

  • Name the subject or the paper, not just "exams".
  • Add the date you will be free.
  • Add a small true complaint: "The syllabus and I are no longer on speaking terms."

On result day

Be gentle. In every group there is someone whose result did not go well. A simple "Done. Thankful." carries your joy without rubbing it in, and you can share the marks privately with the people who asked. If your own result was disappointing, you do not owe anyone a status at all. One exam is one day in a long life, and many people who did badly once went on to do well.

What to avoid

  • Posting photos of admit cards, roll numbers or mark sheets with numbers showing.
  • Jokes about cheating.
  • Comparing ranks in public.
  • Staying on the phone to write the perfect study status. The irony is noted.

Before you post

  • Post the status, then keep the phone in another room. That is the real exam hack.
