Let the photo breathe

A sunset does not need much help. The best nature captions are short and step aside: a place, a time, a single thought. "Kasauli, 6.40 pm" is a complete caption. If you add a line, keep it quieter than the picture.

Match the line to the scene

  • Sunset or sunrise: time and place, or one calm thought.
  • Hills and fog: the cold, the silence, the tea.
  • Fields and villages: the crop, the season, whose land it is.
  • Flowers and gardens: who planted them.
  • Night sky and moon: where you were standing.

Make it yours

Say what your other senses noticed, because the photo only has sight. The smell of wet earth, the sound of a tubewell, the cold of the stone you sat on. "Sarson in bloom, Bebe calling from the house" puts the reader in the field with you.

Take care outdoors

  • Do not step onto cliff edges, railway tracks or into fast water for a photo.
  • Leave flowers on the plant and litter in your bag.
  • In forests and sanctuaries, follow the local rules about distance from animals.

Before you post

  • Add the place and time of day. People like to know where the sky looked like that.
