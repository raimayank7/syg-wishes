When to post a friendship line

On a friend's birthday, on Friendship Day, under an old school photo, after a trip, or on an ordinary day when a friend did something kind. The ordinary day is the best of these, because nobody expects it.

Make it about one friend

General lines about friendship are pleasant. A line that could only be about your friend is a gift. Add the nickname, the canteen, the scooter, the exam you both nearly failed. "Twenty years, one bench, zero secrets" becomes real when the bench was in a real classroom. If you have a group, name the group's own joke and tag nobody: the right people will know.

What to avoid

  • Ranking friends in public. "My only true friend" hurts the other five who read it.
  • Posting a friend's funny or sleeping photo without asking.
  • Using a friendship line to point at a friend you are upset with.

For longer messages to send to a friend directly, see friendship messages, and for 1 August or the first Sunday of August, Friendship Day wishes.

Before you post

  • Post it with an old photo. The worse the photo, the better the status.
