When a life line fits

Lines about life suit the quiet days: a birthday that made you think, the end of a school year, a long train ride, an evening after good news or hard news. They are also the safest kind of text status when you have no photo to post, because they say something without telling anyone your business.

Turn a general line into yours

A life line becomes yours when you tie it to something that happened. Take "Small joys, counted daily" and add the joy: "Small joys, counted daily. Today: first mango of the season." Take a Hindi line about patience and add where you learnt it: in a queue, in a kitchen, in a hostel. One real detail is enough.

What to avoid

  • Giving advice to the world. "Life taught me" reads better than "People should".
  • Very dark lines without context. Friends will worry, and you will spend the evening explaining.
  • Posting a wise line right after a fight. Everyone can see the timing.
  • Long quotations from famous people. If you want real quotations with the source named, see Quotes.

Before you post

  • Post the line that matches your week. A line you believe reads differently from one you only liked.
