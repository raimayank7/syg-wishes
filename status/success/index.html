Success and achievement status

Good news deserves a line that is glad without being loud. These are for results, selections, first salaries, new shops, new keys and every finish line you worked for.

English

  • It took three years to become an overnight success.

  • Result out. Parents smiling. That is the whole news.

  • First salary. First call: home.

  • New keys, old gratitude.

  • Hard work said it would pay. It paid.

  • Signed, sealed and still pinching myself.

  • Promoted. Same person, new chair.

  • Opened the shutter of my own shop today. Bless it.

  • Passed. Sleeping for two days now.

  • This one is for everyone who said "keep going".

  • From "can I?" to "I did". Long road, good view.

  • The degree is mine. The credit is shared.

  • Small step for the world. Huge one for my family.

How to share good news well

The most liked achievement posts all do the same three things. They state the news plainly. They thank someone. They stay short. "Cleared the exam. Thank you Maa, for every 5 am cup of tea." Nobody minds a success told that way, and many will feel a share of it.

Tell people before you tell everyone

Parents, teachers, a mentor, the friend who lent you notes: they should hear it from you in a call or a message before they see it in a status. The status comes last. If the news depends on a formal step, such as a joining letter or a registry, wait until that step is done before you post.

Make it yours

  • Say how long it took: "Third attempt. Worth all three."
  • Thank by name.
  • Mention the first thing you did: called home, went to the gurdwara or temple, slept for twelve hours.

What to avoid

  • Offer letters, appointment letters, mark sheets and ID cards in photos. They carry numbers that can be misused.
  • Salary figures and prices.
  • "To those who doubted me" lines. They make the day about the doubters.
  • Posting during a friend's bad news on the same matter. An hour's delay costs you nothing.

Before you post

  • Thank first, announce second. The order changes how the whole line sounds.

Questions

Is it wrong to post about my own success?

No. People who care about you want to know. Keep it short, thank someone, and leave out figures.
