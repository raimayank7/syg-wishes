Mother's Day wishes

Mother's Day falls on the second Sunday of May in India. A wish that thanks her for one specific thing will be kept longer than any forward.

Wishes

  • Happy Mother's Day, Maa. I am what I am because you never stopped believing in me.

  • Happy Mother's Day, Mummy. Your love is the one thing in my life that never needed a reason.

  • To my mother: thank you for every tiffin, every prayer and every "call me when you reach". Happy Mother's Day.

  • Maa, you gave up so much so quietly that we noticed only later. Happy Mother's Day.

  • Happy Mother's Day to my mother-in-law, who gave me a second home and the person I love.

  • Happy Mother's Day, Dadi. Your stories and your pickles hold this family together.

  • Happy Mother's Day, Nani. Your house is still the happiest address I know.

  • Happy Mother's Day to my wife. Our children are lucky, and so am I.

  • Happy first Mother's Day! You are doing better than you think.

  • You packed my tiffin for twelve years and never once repeated a complaint. Happy Mother's Day, Maa.

  • Everything I know about being kind, I learnt by watching you. Happy Mother's Day.

  • To my mother-in-law: thank you for raising the person I love. Happy Mother's Day.

  • Nani, you were my mother's mother and my best friend. Happy Mother's Day.

Thank her for something she thinks nobody noticed

Mothers are thanked in general and seldom in particular. The wish she will keep is the one that names a thing: the tiffin packed at five in the morning for twelve years, the fees she arranged without telling you how, the way she still asks whether you have eaten. One sentence of that is worth a page of "best mother in the world".

Make it yours

  • Use what you call her, in the language you speak to her in.
  • Add a memory with a year or a place in it.
  • If you live away: call first, send the message after, and say when you are coming home.
  • If she is not on a phone: write it on paper, or say it aloud. The wish is the words, not the app.

Every kind of mother

Mother's Day is also for grandmothers, mothers-in-law, aunts, elder sisters and teachers who mothered you. A wish to a mother-in-law that thanks her for raising the person you married is remembered for years. And spare a thought for those who find the day hard: people who have lost their mothers, mothers who have lost a child, and those who wished to be mothers. For them a quiet "thinking of you today" is the kindest message.

What to avoid

  • A status about her, and nothing said to her.
  • Praise for sacrifice alone. Ask her what she would like to do for herself, and help it happen.

Make it yours

  • Thank her for one exact thing: the tiffin, the late nights, the phone calls.
  • If you live away, call first and send the message after.
