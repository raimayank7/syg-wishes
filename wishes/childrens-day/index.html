Children's Day wishes

India keeps Children's Day on 14 November, the birthday of Jawaharlal Nehru. These wishes are for parents to send their children and for teachers to share with a class.

Wishes

  • Happy Children's Day! Stay curious, ask too many questions and never stop drawing outside the lines.

  • Happy Children's Day to my little one. You teach me something new every day.

  • To all my students: you are the best part of my job. Happy Children's Day!

  • Happy Children's Day! Play hard today. Homework can wait until tomorrow. Do not tell your teacher I said so.

  • Dear children, the world is big and you are welcome in all of it. Happy Children's Day.

  • Happy Children's Day to the child in every grown up too. Go eat an ice cream.

  • Happy Children's Day, beta. Be kind, be brave and be yourself.

  • You asked "why" forty times yesterday. Never stop. Happy Children's Day.

  • To my grandchild: you are the best story I tell. Happy Children's Day.

  • Class 4-B: keep asking questions, and I will keep finding answers. Happy Children's Day.

  • Today the rules are simple: play more, laugh louder, homework later. Happy Children's Day.

  • You shared your tiffin with the new girl. That is the kind of clever I am proudest of.

Wishes children actually like

Children can tell at once whether an adult means it. They do not want to be told they are the future of the nation. They want to hear that you noticed something: the drawing, the question in class, the way they shared their tiffin, the hundredth "why". Tell them that, and add a treat.

Make it yours

  • Parents: one thing your child did this year that made you proud, and it should not be marks.
  • Grandparents: a blessing and a story.
  • Teachers: a line for the whole class, and if you can manage it, one word for each child.
  • Uncles, aunts and elder cousins: a promise of time, such as a match, a film or a cycle ride, kept within the week.

For the class board

A wish for a whole class should be short enough to read from the back bench and warm enough to be copied into diaries: "Class 4-B: keep asking questions. I will keep finding answers. Happy Children's Day." Many schools turn the day around, with teachers performing for the children. A funny wish from a strict teacher is the best gift of all.

The day itself

India keeps Children's Day on 14 November. World Children's Day is observed on 20 November. The day is also a reminder that every child has a right to school, play and safety, and a good moment to give books, toys or time to children who have less.

What to avoid

  • Comparing the child with a sibling or a classmate, even in praise.
  • Advice dressed up as a wish.

Make it yours

  • Children like to be told exactly what you admire: their questions, their drawings, their kindness.
  • For a class, keep it short enough to write on the board.
