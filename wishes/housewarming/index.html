Housewarming and new home wishes

A new home is years of saving and one very big day. These wishes suit a griha pravesh invitation reply, a gift card or a message when you see the first photos.

Wishes

  • Congratulations on your new home! May every room collect good memories.

  • A new address and a new chapter. Wishing you peace, health and happiness in your new home.

  • You built it brick by brick and month by month. Congratulations on your own home!

  • May your new home always smell of good food and sound like laughter. Congratulations!

  • New keys, new walls, same lovely people. Happy housewarming!

  • Wishing you a home where the tea is always ready and the door is always open. Congratulations!

  • Congratulations on your first home. This is a big one. So proud of you.

  • May your new home keep you warm in winter, cool in summer and happy all year. Best wishes.

  • Best wishes for your griha pravesh. May the new home bring good fortune to the whole family.

  • May the kitchen always smell of something good, and the door always open to friends.

  • A home of your own, at last. I know how many years are in those walls. Congratulations.

  • New address, same warm people. May this house be kind to you.

A wish for a home, not a house

A good housewarming wish is about what will happen inside the walls: meals, guests, festivals, children growing, quiet evenings. The building has already been praised by the bank. Wish for the life.

Make it yours

  • If you have visited: praise one real thing, such as the light in the kitchen, the terrace or the neem tree outside the gate.
  • If it is a first home: say you are proud, and that you know what it took.
  • If it is a parents' home built after retirement: wish them rest in it, and promise to visit.
  • For a griha pravesh or an Akhand Path at the new house: respectful words, and thanks for the invitation.

With a gift or without

If you are sending a gift, the card needs only two lines and your name. If you cannot attend, send the wish on the day and say when you will come to see the house. A plant, sweets or something for the kitchen is always suitable. The family will tell you if they would rather have no gifts.

What to avoid

  • Asking the price, the loan or the area in square feet.
  • Advice on what they should have done differently.
  • Comparing it with anyone else's house.

Make it yours

  • If you have seen the house, praise one real thing: the light, the balcony, the kitchen.
  • For a first home, say you are proud. People remember who said it.
