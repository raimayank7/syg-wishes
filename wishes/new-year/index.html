New Year wishes

A New Year wish is best when it is honest about the year that went and hopeful about the one that is coming. Choose warm, funny or formal.

For friends and family

  • Happy New Year! May this year be gentle with you and generous to you.

  • New year, same us, better plans. Happy New Year, my friend!

  • Happy New Year to my family. Whatever the year brings, we will face it at the same dining table.

  • May this year give you fewer worries, more weekends and one thing you have long waited for. Happy New Year!

  • Twelve new chapters are waiting. Write good ones. Happy New Year!

  • Happy New Year! Thank you for being one of the good parts of the last one.

  • Happy New Year! My resolution is the same as last year, because I did not touch it.

For work

  • Wishing you and your family a happy and healthy New Year. Thank you for your support through the year.

  • Happy New Year to the whole team. Thank you for the hard work. Let us make the new year even better.

  • Happy New Year! It was a pleasure working with you this year. Looking forward to more.

  • Season's greetings and best wishes for the New Year. May it bring growth and good health.

Before midnight or after?

At midnight on 31 December everyone's phone is flooded, and most wishes go unread. A wish sent on the afternoon of the 31st, or on the calm morning of 1 January, gets attention. For people abroad, send by their clock.

Make it yours

  • Look back first: one thing you are grateful to the person for in the year that is ending.
  • Then one real hope for them in the new year: the exam, the house, the health, the baby.
  • For a year that was hard on them: do not pretend it was not. "It was a rough one. I hope the new year goes easier on you. I am around."

New Year wishes at work

To clients, customers and seniors, thank them for the past year's trust and wish them well, in two or three lines, signed with your name and firm. Send it individually where you can. A mass message with someone else's name left at the bottom does more harm than no wish.

Other new years

India has several. Baisakhi, Ugadi, Gudi Padwa, Puthandu, Vishu, Poila Boishakh, Bestu Varas after Diwali and others each begin a year for their communities. If a friend keeps one of these, a wish on that day is noticed and remembered.

What to avoid

  • Resolutions suggested for other people.
  • Long forwards with the year's numbers arranged cleverly.

Make it yours

  • For clients and seniors, thank them for the year past before wishing for the next.
  • Skip the long forwards at midnight. A two line wish written for one person is remembered.
