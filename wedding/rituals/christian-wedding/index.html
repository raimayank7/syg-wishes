Christian wedding: rituals in order

Christian weddings in India are held in a church, with vows, rings and hymns, followed by a reception. Communities in Kerala, Goa, Mangaluru, Tamil Nadu and the North-East each add their own customs.

RitualWhenWhat happens
Engagement or betrothalMonths beforeThe families and often the priest witness the couple's promise to marry. Rings may be exchanged.
BannsWeeks beforeIn many churches the intended marriage is announced on Sundays before the wedding.
RoceThe evening beforeIn Goan and Mangalorean Catholic families, the bride and groom are anointed with coconut milk at their homes.
ProcessionAt the churchThe groom waits at the altar. The bride walks down the aisle, usually with her father.
Vows and ringsIn the serviceThe couple make their vows before the priest or pastor and exchange rings.
Minnu or thaliIn the serviceIn Kerala and Tamil Nadu, the groom ties a small gold pendant on a thread, the minnu or thali, around the bride's neck. In Kerala he also gives her a sari, the manthrakodi.
Blessing and signing the registerEnd of the serviceThe priest blesses the couple, and the couple and witnesses sign the marriage register.
ReceptionAfter the serviceCake cutting, a toast to the couple, a first dance in many communities, and a meal.

Customs differ from family to family and from place to place. This is a general guide for guests and for couples learning about each other's traditions, not a rule book. Reviewed on 11 November 2026.

If you are a guest

  • Arrive before the bride. Once the service begins, enter quietly.
  • Stand and sit with the congregation. You need not join prayers that are not your own.
  • Photography during the service may be limited. Follow the church's rules.
  • White is the bride's colour in many communities, so guests usually choose other colours.

Planning notes for the two families

The church

Meet the parish priest or pastor as early as you can. Many churches ask couples to give notice months ahead and to attend marriage preparation sessions, and they will tell you which certificates they need from each of you. Requirements differ between churches, so ask yours for its list.

The service

Choose the readings, the hymns and the choir or musicians with the priest. Decide who walks in the procession: bridesmaids, groomsmen, flower girls, the ring bearer. Hold a rehearsal a day or two before so that everyone knows where to stand.

Rings, register and witnesses

Give the rings to one dependable person. The couple and their witnesses sign the church register after the blessing, so the witnesses must be chosen and present. Ask the church how and when the marriage certificate is issued.

The reception

A reception usually follows with a cake, a toast and the first dance. Brief the master of ceremonies with the order of events and the names he must announce.

Questions people ask

What are banns?

A public announcement of an intended marriage, read in church on Sundays before the wedding, so that anyone who knows of a reason against it can speak.

Is a church wedding legally valid in India?

Christian marriages are solemnised under the Indian Christian Marriage Act, 1872, by a person authorised under that Act, and entered in the marriage register.
