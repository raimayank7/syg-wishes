Marathi wedding: rituals in order

A Maharashtrian wedding is simple, punctual and held in the morning. Its best-known moment is the antarpat, the cloth held between the bride and groom while the mangalashtaka verses are sung.

Sakhar pudaBefore the weddingThe engagement. The groom's family gives the bride a sari and a packet of sugar.

KanyadaanAfter the garlandsThe bride's parents give their daughter to the groom.

Varat and griha praveshAfter the weddingThe bride leaves for the groom's home, where she enters by gently tipping over a measure of rice at the door.

Customs differ from family to family and from place to place. This is a general guide for guests and for couples learning about each other's traditions, not a rule book. Reviewed on 11 November 2026.

If you are a guest

  • Be seated before the muhurta. Guests are given akshata (coloured rice) to shower on the couple when the antarpat is removed.
  • The bride often wears a yellow or green sari in the nauvari style, with green bangles and the mundavalya, a string of pearls across the forehead that the groom also wears.
  • The meal is vegetarian in many families and is served soon after the ceremony.

Planning notes for the two families

The muhurta

A Marathi wedding runs to the minute. The antarpat is removed and the garlands exchanged exactly at the muhurta, usually in the morning. Print the time on the invitation and ask guests to be seated before it.

Akshata and the mangalashtaka

Arrange enough akshata, the coloured rice, for every guest, and people to hand it out as guests enter. Decide who will sing the mangalashtaka: the priest, a family member or relatives who have written a verse for the couple.

Things to arrange

The antarpat cloth, the mundavalya for both bride and groom, the green bangles, the items the guruji lists for the lajahoma, and the gifts for the seemant puja, when the bride's family welcomes the groom.

The meal

Lunch follows soon after the ceremony. With a morning muhurta, many families also serve breakfast to early guests, so tell the caterer the true arrival time, not only the muhurta.

Questions people ask

What is the antarpat?

A cloth held between the bride and the groom so that they do not see each other until the auspicious moment, when it is removed.

What are mangalashtaka?

Eight verses of blessing sung at the wedding. Guests join in the last words and shower rice on the couple.
