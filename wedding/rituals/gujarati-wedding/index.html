Gujarati wedding: rituals in order

A Gujarati wedding is lively and full of small playful rituals, with garba the night before. The ceremony has four pheras, called the mangal phera.

RitualWhenWhat happens
Chandlo matli and gol dhanaBefore the weddingThe families accept the match. At the gol dhana (engagement), coriander seeds and jaggery are shared.
Mandap mahurat and Ganesh pujaA day or two beforePrayers at each home to begin the wedding.
PithiA day or two beforeA paste of turmeric and sandalwood is applied to the bride and the groom.
Garba and sangeetThe night beforeEveryone dances garba and dandiya.
Jaan and ponkhvuWedding dayThe groom's procession arrives. The bride's mother welcomes him and playfully tries to catch his nose.
JaimalaStart of the ceremonyThe couple exchange garlands.
Kanyadaan and hasta melapIn the mandapThe bride's parents give her hand to the groom, and the couple's hands and scarves are tied together.
Mangal pheraIn the mandapThe couple walk four times around the fire, standing for dharma, artha, kama and moksha.
SaptapadiIn the mandapThe couple take seven steps and seven vows.
Saubhagyavati bhava and vidaaiEnd of the weddingMarried women bless the bride, and she takes leave of her family.

Customs differ from family to family and from place to place. This is a general guide for guests and for couples learning about each other's traditions, not a rule book. Reviewed on 11 November 2026.

If you are a guest

  • Bring energy for the garba night, and comfortable shoes.
  • During the wedding the bride's sisters steal the groom's shoes and return them for a ransom. Guests enjoy the bargaining.
  • The food is vegetarian.
  • Bright bandhani and patola colours are at home here.

Planning notes for the two families

The garba night

The garba or sangeet evening needs a floor large enough for circles, a sound system or live singers, dandiya sticks for guests and drinking water close at hand. Tell outstation guests the dress code in advance, because many will want to wear chaniya choli and kediyu.

The jaan and the ponkhvu

Agree on when the jaan, the groom's procession, will arrive, and who from the bride's family will perform the ponkhvu, the welcome of the groom at the entrance.

The mandap

The priest (maharaj) will give the list for the Ganesh puja and the mandap mahurat. The hasta melap is held at the chosen time, so the photographer and both families should know that hour. Keep a place for the couple's shoes that the bride's sisters do not know about, or accept the ransom with good humour.

Food

The meal is vegetarian and many families ask for dishes without onion and garlic, or a separate Jain counter. Confirm this in writing with the caterer.

Questions people ask

How many pheras in a Gujarati wedding?

Four, called the mangal phera, followed by the seven steps of the saptapadi.

What is gol dhana?

The Gujarati engagement. The name means jaggery and coriander seeds, which are shared at the ceremony.
