Muslim wedding (Nikah): rituals in order

The heart of a Muslim wedding is the nikah, a marriage contract made with the free consent of the bride and the groom before witnesses. The nikah itself is brief. Customs before and after it vary widely across India.

RitualWhenWhat happens
MangniBefore the weddingThe engagement. Families exchange gifts and often rings.
Manjha (haldi)A few days beforeTurmeric paste is applied to the bride, who traditionally wears yellow.
MehndiA day or two beforeHenna is applied to the bride's hands and feet, with songs.
BaraatWedding dayThe groom arrives with his family and friends and is welcomed by the bride's family.
NikahWedding dayThe qazi conducts the nikah. The bride and the groom are each asked for their consent, and each answers "qubool hai". This offer and acceptance (ijab and qubool) takes place before witnesses.
MehrAt the nikahThe mehr is the gift promised by the groom to the bride. It is hers by right, and the amount is written in the nikahnama.
NikahnamaAt the nikahThe marriage contract is signed by the couple, the witnesses and the qazi.
Khutbah and duaAfter the nikahA short sermon is read and prayers are offered for the couple.
RukhsatiAfter the weddingThe bride takes leave of her family.
WalimaAfter the weddingA feast hosted by the groom's family to announce and celebrate the marriage.

Customs differ from family to family and from place to place. This is a general guide for guests and for couples learning about each other's traditions, not a rule book. Reviewed on 11 November 2026.

If you are a guest

  • Dress modestly. Many women guests cover their heads during the nikah.
  • Men and women may be seated separately. Follow the lead of the hosts.
  • The food is a highlight. Wait to be invited to the table.
  • The greeting for the couple is "Mubarak ho".

Planning notes for the two families

The qazi and the witnesses

Arrange the qazi or imam who will conduct the nikah and confirm the date, time and place with him. The nikah needs the consent of the bride and the groom and is witnessed, so settle in advance who the witnesses and the bride's representative (wali or wakil) will be.

The mehr

The mehr is agreed between the families before the day and written into the nikahnama. Discuss it calmly and early, so that it is not negotiated in front of guests.

The nikahnama

Ask who will bring the nikahnama, how many copies will be signed and who keeps them. Keep photocopies of identity documents ready if the qazi asks for them. Store the signed nikahnama safely: it is the record of the marriage. For civil registration, see our page on marriage registration.

Seating and the walima

Decide whether men and women will sit separately at the nikah and tell the decorator and caterer. The walima is the groom's family's feast after the wedding and is planned like a reception, with its own guest list.

Questions people ask

What is mehr?

A gift, usually money, that the groom gives or promises to the bride at the time of the nikah. It belongs to her alone.

What is the walima?

The wedding feast given by the groom or his family after the nikah.
