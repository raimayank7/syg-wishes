Wedding messages: save the date, announcements and thank-you notes

20 short messages for the moments around a wedding that are not the invitation itself: asking people to keep the date free, announcing a marriage afterwards, and thanking guests, family and vendors. Replace the words in brackets and tap Copy.

Save the date

  • Save the date! [Name] and [Name] are getting married on [date] in [city]. Invitation to follow.
  • Mark your calendar and book your tickets: [date], [city]. We are getting married and we want you there.
  • तारीख नोट कर लीजिए: [date]. [Name] और [Name] की शादी है। निमंत्रण जल्द भेजेंगे।

Announcing the wedding afterwards

  • We are happy to share that [Name] and [Name] were married on [date] in a small family ceremony. We ask for your blessings.
  • It was a small wedding, but our happiness is large. [Name] and [Name] are now married. Please keep us in your prayers.
  • आप सबके आशीर्वाद से [Name] और [Name] का विवाह [date] को संपन्न हुआ। अपना स्नेह बनाए रखिए।

Thank you to guests

  • Thank you for being part of our wedding. Your presence and blessings made the day complete.
  • We are still smiling. Thank you for travelling so far, for dancing so much, and for the lovely gift.
  • Thank you for your blessings and your thoughtful gift. We will remember it every time we use it.
  • हमारी शादी में आकर आपने हमारी खुशी दुगनी कर दी। आपके आशीर्वाद और प्यार के लिए दिल से धन्यवाद।
  • आपके स्नेह और शुभकामनाओं के लिए हम दोनों आभारी हैं।

Thank you to those who could not come

  • We missed you at the wedding, and we understand. Thank you for your wishes and your blessings from afar.
  • Thank you for the gift and the kind message. We hope to see you soon and tell you all about it.

Thank you to parents and family

  • Maa, Papa: everything good about that day was your work. Thank you for a wedding we will remember all our lives.
  • To my brothers, sisters and cousins: thank you for the dances, the running around and the sleepless nights. You made it happen.
  • To our friends who handled everything quietly behind the scenes: we noticed. Thank you.

Welcome note for guests' rooms

  • Welcome! We are so glad you are here. Rest well, eat well, and keep your dancing shoes ready. With love, [Names]
  • Thank you for coming all this way for us. The schedule is inside. If you need anything, call [name] on [number].

Thank you to vendors

  • Thank you for the beautiful work at our wedding. Everyone noticed, and we are grateful. We will gladly recommend you.
  • The food was the talk of the wedding. Thank you to you and your whole team.

Writing a thank-you note that feels personal

  • Name the gift or the thing the person did. "Thank you for the dinner set" is warmer than "thank you for the gift".
  • Say how you will use it or what you remember of them at the wedding.
  • Send it within a month. Late thanks are still better than none.
  • For elders, a phone call or a handwritten line means more than a typed message.
