Wedding day bag and emergency kit

36 small things that save the day when a hook breaks, a shoe bites or a phone dies. Pack the bag two days before and give it to one calm person to carry.

Who carries it

Not the bride and not the groom. Choose a sister, a cousin or a friend who will stay close all day, and tell the couple and the parents who that person is. A second small pouch with pins, tissues and lipstick can stay in the bride's room.

One bag for each side

The groom's side needs its own, smaller kit: safety pins for the sehra and the stole, a spare kurta button, tissues, a comb, a charger and the envelopes for the rituals.
