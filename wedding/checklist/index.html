How to use the checklist

  • Share the work. Give whole sections to a parent, a sibling or a friend, and let them own it.
  • If you start late, do not panic. Do the first three sections in one fortnight, starting with the venue.
  • Leave out what does not apply to your wedding. Not every family holds every function.

Questions people ask

When should wedding planning start?

Nine to twelve months before is comfortable, mainly because good venues and photographers are booked that early for the wedding season. A wedding can be planned in three months if you are flexible about the venue and the date.

What should be booked first?

The date and the venue, together. Almost every other booking depends on them.

Is my checklist saved?

Yes, in this browser on this device. It is not sent anywhere. If you clear your browser data or change phones, the ticks are lost, so print the list if you want a paper copy.
