Christmas wishes

Christmas wishes are about warmth, giving and being together. These fit a card, a gift tag or a quick message on the day.

Wishes

  • Merry Christmas! May your home be warm, your plate be full and your people be near.

  • Wishing you a Christmas full of small joys: cake, carols and time with family.

  • Merry Christmas to you and yours. May the season bring peace to your home.

  • Merry Christmas! I hope the day is as sweet as the plum cake.

  • May the spirit of giving stay with us long after the lights come down. Merry Christmas.

  • Merry Christmas, my friend. You are one of the gifts I did not have to unwrap.

  • Merry Christmas and a Happy New Year. Thank you for a wonderful year of working together.

  • Merry Christmas to my favourite teacher. Thank you for all you give us through the year.

  • Sending love across the miles this Christmas. Wish we were at the same table.

Make it yours
