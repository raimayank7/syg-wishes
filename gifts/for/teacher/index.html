Gift ideas for a teacher

A gift for a teacher is a thank you, so keep it modest. Many schools do not allow teachers to accept costly gifts, and a heartfelt note is always allowed.

  • A handwritten letter

    Say three specific things you are grateful for. It is kept longer than any object.

    TokenHandmade

  • A box of their favourite sweets

    Find out the exact shop and the exact sweet. Precision is the gift.

    TokenFood

  • A potted plant

    Pick a hardy one that survives missed waterings: money plant, snake plant or jade.

    TokenPractical

  • A bunch of fresh flowers

    Ask the florist what came in today. Fresh and simple beats large and tired.

    TokenTraditional

  • A good pen

    For someone who still writes by hand. Have the name engraved if the shop offers it.

    TokenPractical

  • A bar of fine chocolate or a small chocolate box

    A safe choice when you do not know someone well.

    TokenFood

  • A book you loved

    Write a line on the first page saying why you chose it for them.

    TokenPersonal

  • A set of diyas or scented candles

    Hand-painted clay diyas suit the season and support a local potter.

    TokenTraditional

  • A jar of homemade pickle, jam or laddus

    Label it with the date and what is inside. Homemade food carries your time in it.

    TokenHandmade

  • A desk organiser or good notebook

    Neutral, useful and right for the office.

    TokenPractical

  • A desk plant in a ceramic pot

    Something alive on the desk. Choose one that needs little light.

    TokenPractical

  • A book of messages from everyone

    Pass a notebook around quietly, or collect messages on the phone and print them.

    TokenHandmade

  • A homemade cake or batch of cookies

    Bake their favourite flavour and pack it in a tin they can keep.

    TokenHandmade

  • A hand-painted card

    Children's drawings are the best cards a grandparent can receive.

    TokenHandmade

  • A plum cake or homemade sweets

    The traditional Christmas gift to neighbours and friends.

    TokenFood

  • A steel water bottle or lunch box

    Sturdy, daily and impossible to dislike.

    TokenPractical

  • A pack of dry fruits

    Keeps for weeks, suits every age, and nobody has to guess a size.

    ModestFood

  • A warm shawl or stole

    Wool for the north, light cotton or silk for the south.

    ModestPractical

  • A gift card for a shop they use

    Less personal, never wasted. Good when you live far away.

    ModestPractical

  • A hamper of snacks and tea

    Build it yourself in a reusable basket. Choose things the person actually eats.

    ModestFood

  • A subscription to a magazine or newspaper

    A gift that arrives again every month.

    ModestPractical

  • A donation in their name

    To a cause they care about. Give them the receipt in a card.

    ModestPersonal

  • A fruit basket

    Seasonal fruit, well arranged. A good gift for elders and for visits to the unwell.

Your shortlist

How to choose for a teacher

  • A handwritten letter from the student, saying what they learnt, is what teachers keep for years.
  • Useful desk and classroom things are appreciated.
  • A class gift from all the students together avoids competition between parents.

About the spending levels

This site does not quote prices, because prices change with the city, the shop and the year. Each idea is marked with one of four levels instead:

  • Token: A small gesture: the cost of a snack or a few cups of tea.
  • Modest: An everyday gift for friends and family.
  • Generous: A special gift for someone close or a big occasion.
  • Grand: A once-in-years gift, often given by a family together.

Questions people ask

What is a good gift for a teacher?

Start from what the person does every day and what they would not buy for themselves. On this page, "A handwritten letter" and "A book of messages from everyone" are two ideas that suit most people.

What if I have very little to spend?

Choose the Token level in the filter. Handmade and personal gifts, such as a handwritten letter, cost little and are often the ones remembered.
