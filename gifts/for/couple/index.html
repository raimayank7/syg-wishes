Gift ideas for a married couple

For a couple, give something they will use together, or money in an envelope, which is the most common and most useful wedding gift in India.

  • A box of their favourite sweets

    Find out the exact shop and the exact sweet. Precision is the gift.

    TokenFood

  • A potted plant

    Pick a hardy one that survives missed waterings: money plant, snake plant or jade.

    TokenPractical

  • A set of diyas or scented candles

    Hand-painted clay diyas suit the season and support a local potter.

    TokenTraditional

  • A tree or fruit sapling

    For someone with a garden. Planted on the day, it marks the year.

    TokenPersonal

  • A doormat, name plate or set of wind chimes

    Small things that make a new house feel finished.

    TokenPractical

  • A pack of dry fruits

    Keeps for weeks, suits every age, and nobody has to guess a size.

    ModestFood

  • A board game for the whole family

    Choose one that grandparents and children can play together.

    ModestPractical

  • A large printed family photograph

    Get everyone into one picture, print it big and frame it.

    ModestPersonal

  • A photo album you have filled yourself

    Printed photos, with dates and names written underneath, in an album they can hold.

    ModestHandmade

  • A set of bath towels

    Thick, plain and in pairs.

    ModestPractical

  • A tea or coffee set

    For a home that loves guests.

    ModestPractical

  • A wall clock or table lamp

    Simple designs suit more homes. Note that some people consider a clock an unlucky gift.

    ModestPractical

  • A toolkit for the house

    Every new home needs a hammer, a screwdriver set and a measuring tape.

    ModestPractical

  • Money in an envelope (shagun)

    Welcome on almost every family occasion, and the giver never has to guess a size or a taste. By custom the amount ends in one.

    ModestTraditional

  • A gift card for a shop they use

    Less personal, never wasted. Good when you live far away.

    ModestPractical

  • A hamper of snacks and tea

    Build it yourself in a reusable basket. Choose things the person actually eats.

    ModestFood

  • A cooking spice box filled with fresh spices

    A masala dabba for a new kitchen.

    ModestPractical

  • A set of storage jars

    A new kitchen never has enough.

    ModestPractical

  • A silver coin

    A traditional token of good fortune at Diwali, weddings and the birth of a child.

    GenerousTraditional

  • Dinner at the place they keep mentioning

    Book the table, arrange the travel, and pay before the bill arrives.

    GenerousExperience

  • A good pressure cooker, mixer or non-stick pan

    Only if they have said they need one. Then buy the best you can.

    GenerousPractical

  • A dinner set or a set of glasses

    Plain white or clear glass fits any home.

    GenerousPractical

  • Bed linen in plain colours

    Check the bed size. Good cotton in a quiet colour is always used.

    GenerousPractical

  • A brass or silver item for the puja room

    A diya, a bell or a small plate. Only when you know the family's faith and practice.

    GenerousTraditional

  • A contribution to their honeymoon or new home

    Ask a family member how the couple would like to receive it.

    GenerousPractical

  • A piece of gold or silver jewellery

    Small and classic, such as earrings, a chain or a ring, is worn more often than large and elaborate.

    GrandTraditional

  • A weekend trip

    Choose a place a few hours away. Two calm days are better than a rushed long journey.

    GrandExperience

  • A big family gift bought together

    A television, a washing machine or a holiday, given by all the children or all the friends as one gift.

Your shortlist

How to choose for a married couple

  • Ask a close family member whether the couple has a wish list or would prefer money.
  • For a new home, choose things that match any decor: plain, good quality, in neutral colours.
  • If you give money, put it in a proper envelope with your name and a line of blessing.

About the spending levels

This site does not quote prices, because prices change with the city, the shop and the year. Each idea is marked with one of four levels instead:

  • Token: A small gesture: the cost of a snack or a few cups of tea.
  • Modest: An everyday gift for friends and family.
  • Generous: A special gift for someone close or a big occasion.
  • Grand: A once-in-years gift, often given by a family together.

Questions people ask

What is a good gift for a married couple?

Start from what the person does every day and what they would not buy for themselves. On this page, "A box of their favourite sweets" and "A gift card for a shop they use" are two ideas that suit most people.

What if I have very little to spend?

Choose the Token level in the filter. Handmade and personal gifts, such as a tree or fruit sapling, cost little and are often the ones remembered.
