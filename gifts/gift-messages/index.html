Gift messages for tags and cards

A gift without a line of writing is only an object. These short messages fit on a tag or the inside of a card. Tap Copy, or better, write one out by hand.

Birthday

  • Happy birthday! I hope this year gives you more of everything you love.
  • For the person who remembers everyone's birthday: today we remember yours.
  • जन्मदिन की ढेरों शुभकामनाएँ। यह छोटा सा तोहफ़ा, बहुत सारे प्यार के साथ।

Wedding

  • Wishing you both a lifetime of love, patience and laughter.
  • May your home always be full of guests, food and happiness. Congratulations!
  • नए जीवन की हार्दिक शुभकामनाएँ। सदा सुखी रहो।

Anniversary

  • To the two of you, who make it look easy. Happy anniversary.
  • Another year together, and still our favourite couple.
  • सालगिरह मुबारक। आपका साथ यूँ ही बना रहे।

Housewarming

  • May this home be filled with good food, good sleep and good people.
  • New walls, new memories. Congratulations on your new home.
  • नए घर की बहुत बधाई। यह घर सदा खुशियों से भरा रहे।

New baby

  • Welcome to the world, little one. You have chosen your parents well.
  • Congratulations! May the baby bring you joy and, one day, a full night's sleep.
  • नन्हे मेहमान के आने की बहुत बधाई।

Thank you

  • A small thank you for a big kindness.
  • You went out of your way for us, and we noticed. Thank you.
  • आपकी मदद के लिए दिल से धन्यवाद।

Farewell and retirement

  • This place will not be the same without you. Go well, and stay in touch.
  • Thank you for everything you taught us. Enjoy every free morning.
  • आपके साथ काम करना सौभाग्य रहा। नई पारी के लिए शुभकामनाएँ।

Diwali and festivals

  • Wishing you light, sweets and good company this Diwali.
  • Eid Mubarak to you and your family.
  • Merry Christmas and a happy New Year!
  • दीपावली की हार्दिक शुभकामनाएँ।

Writing your own in three lines

  1. Name the occasion: "Happy 60th birthday, Papa."
  2. Say one true thing: a memory, a quality you admire, or why you chose this gift.
  3. End with a wish and your name.

Three honest lines are better than a long verse copied from somewhere.
