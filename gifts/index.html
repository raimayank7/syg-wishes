About the spending levels

This site does not quote prices, because prices change with the city, the shop and the year. Each idea is marked with one of four levels instead:

  • Token: A small gesture: the cost of a snack or a few cups of tea.
  • Modest: An everyday gift for friends and family.
  • Generous: A special gift for someone close or a big occasion.
  • Grand: A once-in-years gift, often given by a family together.

Questions people ask

Why are there no prices?

Prices differ by city, shop and season and go out of date quickly. Each idea has a spending level in words instead, from Token to Grand.

Do you sell these gifts?

No. This site sells nothing and has no shop links. The ideas are suggestions to take to any shop you like.

What is the best gift when I have very little money?

Something made or written by you: a letter, a framed photograph, a meal you cook. Choose "Token" and "Handmade" in the finder.

Is it rude to give money?

Not in India. Money in an envelope, called shagun, is the usual gift at weddings and many family occasions.
