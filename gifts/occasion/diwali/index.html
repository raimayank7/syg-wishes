Diwali gift ideas

Diwali gifts are exchanged between families, neighbours, friends and business contacts. They are tokens of good wishes, usually sweets, dry fruits and things for the home.

  • A box of their favourite sweets

    Find out the exact shop and the exact sweet. Precision is the gift.

    TokenFood

  • A potted plant

    Pick a hardy one that survives missed waterings: money plant, snake plant or jade.

    TokenPractical

  • A set of diyas or scented candles

    Hand-painted clay diyas suit the season and support a local potter.

    TokenTraditional

  • A jar of homemade pickle, jam or laddus

    Label it with the date and what is inside. Homemade food carries your time in it.

  • A storybook or a set of picture books

Your shortlist

Choosing a Diwali gift

  • Dry fruits keep longer than milk sweets, which matters when a family receives many boxes.
  • Diyas, candles and things for the puja suit the festival.
  • For staff and helpers, money or something truly useful is kinder than a decorative item.

About the spending levels

This site does not quote prices, because prices change with the city, the shop and the year. Each idea is marked with one of four levels instead:

  • Token: A small gesture: the cost of a snack or a few cups of tea.
  • Modest: An everyday gift for friends and family.
  • Generous: A special gift for someone close or a big occasion.
  • Grand: A once-in-years gift, often given by a family together.

Questions people ask

What is a good Diwali gift?

Dry fruits keep longer than milk sweets, which matters when a family receives many boxes. Two ideas from this page: "A potted plant" and "A warm shawl or stole".

Is it fine to give money?

In India, money in an envelope is a normal and welcome gift on most family occasions, especially weddings and festivals. Add a line of blessing and your name. See our page on shagun.
