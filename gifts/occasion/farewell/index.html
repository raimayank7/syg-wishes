Farewell gift ideas

When a colleague or classmate leaves, the gift is a memory to carry. It should be easy to pack.

  • A handwritten letter

    Say three specific things you are grateful for. It is kept longer than any object.

    TokenHandmade

  • A framed photograph of the two of you

    Choose a photo they have not seen for years. Print it larger than you think.

    TokenPersonal

  • A box of their favourite sweets

    Find out the exact shop and the exact sweet. Precision is the gift.

    TokenFood

  • A potted plant

    Pick a hardy one that survives missed waterings: money plant, snake plant or jade.

    TokenPractical

  • A good pen

    For someone who still writes by hand. Have the name engraved if the shop offers it.

    TokenPractical

  • A bar of fine chocolate or a small chocolate box

    A safe choice when you do not know someone well.

    TokenFood

  • A book you loved

    Write a line on the first page saying why you chose it for them.

    TokenPersonal

  • A photo collage printed as a poster

    Collect pictures from friends and family first, then join them in any collage maker and print the result.

    TokenHandmade

  • A keychain with a name or a small photo

    Useful every day and impossible to have too many of.

    TokenPersonal

  • A desk organiser or good notebook

    Neutral, useful and right for the office.

    TokenPractical

  • A desk plant in a ceramic pot

    Something alive on the desk. Choose one that needs little light.

    TokenPractical

  • A mug with a line only your team understands

    Inside jokes make the best farewell gifts.

    TokenPersonal

  • A book of messages from everyone

    Pass a notebook around quietly, or collect messages on the phone and print them.

    TokenHandmade

  • A homemade cake or batch of cookies

    Bake their favourite flavour and pack it in a tin they can keep.

    TokenHandmade

  • A good umbrella or raincoat

    Unromantic and thanked for every monsoon.

    TokenPractical

  • A steel water bottle or lunch box

    Sturdy, daily and impossible to dislike.

  • A gift card for a shop they use

    Less personal, never wasted. Good when you live far away.

    Accessories need no size.

Your shortlist

Choosing a farewell gift

  • A card or book signed by everyone matters more than the object.
  • Choose something small and light if the person is moving cities.
  • Collect a little from everyone for one good gift.

About the spending levels

This site does not quote prices, because prices change with the city, the shop and the year. Each idea is marked with one of four levels instead:

  • Token: A small gesture: the cost of a snack or a few cups of tea.
  • Modest: An everyday gift for friends and family.
  • Generous: A special gift for someone close or a big occasion.
  • Grand: A once-in-years gift, often given by a family together.

Questions people ask

What is a good farewell gift?

A card or book signed by everyone matters more than the object. Two ideas from this page: "A framed photograph of the two of you" and "A mug with a line only your team understands".

Is it fine to give money?

In India, money in an envelope is a normal and welcome gift on most family occasions, especially weddings and festivals. Add a line of blessing and your name. See our page on shagun.
