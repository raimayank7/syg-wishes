Your shortlist

Choosing a Raksha Bandhan gift

  • An envelope with a small personal gift is the usual combination.
  • When the rakhi is sent by post, send the gift early or transfer it with a message on the day.
  • Sisters giving to brothers: sweets with the rakhi are traditional, and a small useful gift is a welcome surprise.

About the spending levels

This site does not quote prices, because prices change with the city, the shop and the year. Each idea is marked with one of four levels instead:

  • Token: A small gesture: the cost of a snack or a few cups of tea.
  • Modest: An everyday gift for friends and family.
  • Generous: A special gift for someone close or a big occasion.
  • Grand: A once-in-years gift, often given by a family together.

Questions people ask

What is a good Raksha Bandhan gift?

An envelope with a small personal gift is the usual combination. Two ideas from this page: "A box of their favourite sweets" and "Sports gear for their game".

Is it fine to give money?

In India, money in an envelope is a normal and welcome gift on most family occasions, especially weddings and festivals. Add a line of blessing and your name. See our page on shagun.
