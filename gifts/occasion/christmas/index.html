Christmas gift ideas

Christmas gifts are exchanged within the family and among friends, often placed under the tree, and many offices and classes play Secret Santa.

  • A bar of fine chocolate or a small chocolate box

    A safe choice when you do not know someone well.

    TokenFood

  • A book you loved

    Write a line on the first page saying why you chose it for them.

    TokenPersonal

  • A jar of homemade pickle, jam or laddus

    Label it with the date and what is inside. Homemade food carries your time in it.

    TokenHandmade

  • A storybook or a set of picture books

    Ask the bookseller for the right reading age. Write the child's name and the date inside.

    TokenPractical

  • Crayons, paints and a drawing book

    Add a large roll of plain paper. Children run out of paper before colours.

    TokenPractical

  • A desk organiser or good notebook

    Neutral, useful and right for the office.

    TokenPractical

  • A desk plant in a ceramic pot

    Something alive on the desk. Choose one that needs little light.

    Inside jokes make the best farewell gifts.

  • A hand-knitted or hand-stitched item

    A scarf, a cushion cover, an embroidered handkerchief.

    TokenHandmade

  • A homemade cake or batch of cookies

    Bake their favourite flavour and pack it in a tin they can keep.

    TokenHandmade

  • A plum cake or homemade sweets

    The traditional Christmas gift to neighbours and friends.

    TokenFood

  • A small wrapped gift within the agreed limit

    For Secret Santa: something useful, amusing and unisex if you do not know the person.

Choosing a Christmas gift

  • Homemade cake, cookies and sweets are the traditional gift to neighbours and friends.
  • For Secret Santa, stay inside the spending limit the group agreed. Our name drawer can pair everyone fairly.
  • Wrap it well. At Christmas the wrapping is half the pleasure.

About the spending levels

This site does not quote prices, because prices change with the city, the shop and the year. Each idea is marked with one of four levels instead:

  • Token: A small gesture: the cost of a snack or a few cups of tea.
  • Modest: An everyday gift for friends and family.
  • Generous: A special gift for someone close or a big occasion.
  • Grand: A once-in-years gift, often given by a family together.

Questions people ask

What is a good Christmas gift?

Homemade cake, cookies and sweets are the traditional gift to neighbours and friends. Two ideas from this page: "A book you loved" and "A plum cake or homemade sweets".

Is it fine to give money?

In India, money in an envelope is a normal and welcome gift on most family occasions, especially weddings and festivals. Add a line of blessing and your name. See our page on shagun.
