Wedding gift ideas

Your shortlist

Choosing a wedding gift

  • By custom the amount ends in one, such as 101, 501 or 1,101, because the extra rupee is considered auspicious. The amount itself depends on your closeness and your means.
  • Write your name on the envelope. The family keeps a record of who gave what.
  • If you give an object, choose something for the home that does not depend on taste, and include an exchange slip if possible.

About the spending levels

This site does not quote prices, because prices change with the city, the shop and the year. Each idea is marked with one of four levels instead:

  • Token: A small gesture: the cost of a snack or a few cups of tea.
  • Modest: An everyday gift for friends and family.
  • Generous: A special gift for someone close or a big occasion.
  • Grand: A once-in-years gift, often given by a family together.

Questions people ask

What is a good wedding gift?

By custom the amount ends in one, such as 101, 501 or 1,101, because the extra rupee is considered auspicious. The amount itself depends on your closeness and your means. Two ideas from this page: "A tea or coffee set" and "A silver coin".

Is it fine to give money?

In India, money in an envelope is a normal and welcome gift on most family occasions, especially weddings and festivals. Add a line of blessing and your name. See our page on shagun.
