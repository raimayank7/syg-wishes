Wedding anniversary gift ideas

An anniversary gift celebrates two people and their years together. For your own spouse, make it personal. For parents or friends, give something they can enjoy as a couple.

  • A handwritten letter

    Say three specific things you are grateful for. It is kept longer than any object.

    TokenHandmade

  • A framed photograph of the two of you

    Choose a photo they have not seen for years. Print it larger than you think.

    TokenPersonal

  • A bunch of fresh flowers

    Ask the florist what came in today. Fresh and simple beats large and tired.

    TokenTraditional

  • A photo collage printed as a poster

    Collect pictures from friends and family first, then join them in any collage maker and print the result.

Your shortlist

Choosing a wedding anniversary gift

  • For parents' silver or golden jubilee, a gift from all the children together, such as a trip, a party or a piece of jewellery, means more than separate gifts.
  • Old photographs, restored and framed, are the classic anniversary gift.
  • For your own anniversary, repeat something from your early days: the same restaurant, the same film, the same walk.

About the spending levels

This site does not quote prices, because prices change with the city, the shop and the year. Each idea is marked with one of four levels instead:

  • Token: A small gesture: the cost of a snack or a few cups of tea.
  • Modest: An everyday gift for friends and family.
  • Generous: A special gift for someone close or a big occasion.
  • Grand: A once-in-years gift, often given by a family together.

Questions people ask

What is a good wedding anniversary gift?

For parents' silver or golden jubilee, a gift from all the children together, such as a trip, a party or a piece of jewellery, means more than separate gifts. Two ideas from this page: "A framed photograph of the two of you" and "Earrings or a bracelet".

Is it fine to give money?

In India, money in an envelope is a normal and welcome gift on most family occasions, especially weddings and festivals. Add a line of blessing and your name. See our page on shagun.
