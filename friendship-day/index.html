Friendship Day wishes

In India Friendship Day is kept on the first Sunday of August. Friends do not need fine words. They need to know you remember.

Wishes

  • Happy Friendship Day! Thank you for knowing me this well and staying anyway.

  • To my best friend: we do not talk every day, but when we do, no time has passed. Happy Friendship Day.

  • Happy Friendship Day to the group that turns bad days into funny stories.

  • Friends are the family we pick up along the way. I picked well. Happy Friendship Day!

  • Happy Friendship Day to my school friends. Different cities, same bench in my head.

  • To my office friend: you are the reason Mondays are bearable. Happy Friendship Day.

  • Happy Friendship Day! Let us meet this month. No "soon". A date.

  • A good friend listens to the same story three times and laughs each time. Thank you. Happy Friendship Day.

  • हैप्पी फ्रेंडशिप डे, दोस्त। रिश्ते खून के नहीं, भरोसे के होते हैं।

  • दोस्त वही जो मुश्किल में साथ दे और खुशी में टाँग खींचे। फ्रेंडशिप डे की बधाई!
