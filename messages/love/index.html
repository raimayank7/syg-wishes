Love messages

Love is said best in ordinary words on ordinary days. These messages are clean, sincere and short enough to send in the middle of a working day.

Everyday

  • No reason for this message. I was thinking of you and wanted you to know.

  • Home is not the address. It is wherever you are waiting.

  • Thank you for today: the tea, the patience, the way you laughed at dinner.

  • I like who I am when I am with you. That is your doing.

  • You handled a lot today. I saw it. Proud of you, and coming home early.

  • After all these years, your call is still the one I pick up fastest.

  • Eat on time, drink water and come home soon. This is a love letter. It only looks like instructions.

Long distance

  • Different cities, same moon, same thoughts. Good night from far away.

  • The best part of my day is still the ten minutes we talk. Do not hang up early today.

  • The distance is temporary. What we have is not.

For husband or wife

  • We have built a home, raised worries and paid bills together. I would not want any other partner for any of it.

  • You are my favourite person to do nothing with.

  • I do not say it enough: I am lucky. Thank you for being mine.

  • Growing old does not scare me, as long as it is next to you.

Everyday beats grand

Couples who last are usually not the ones who write poems on anniversaries. They are the ones who send "reached?", "eaten?" and "saw this and thought of you" on ordinary days. A love message is attention put into words. The more particular the attention, the better the message.

Make it yours

  • Notice something from today: "You handled that call with your mother so gently."
  • Thank them for something invisible: the bills paid, the school run, the patience.
  • Recall a shared moment in five words: "Shimla, the broken umbrella, us."
  • Use your own names for each other. They are worth more than any borrowed line.

When words matter most

After a hard day at their work. Before something that frightens them. When they are travelling. On the morning after a quarrel, along with "sorry". And on a perfectly normal afternoon, which is when a message is least expected and most kept.

A word of care

These messages are for a partner who welcomes them. If you are writing to someone who has not shown interest, or who has asked for space, the respectful message is none. Love messages are private too: think twice before putting them in a status or a group where family and colleagues will read them. For verse, see our short love poems.

Make it yours

  • Send one on a normal Tuesday. A message with no occasion means the most.
  • Mention something they did today or this week. Attention is the real compliment.
