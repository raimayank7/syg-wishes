Group announcement messages

A good announcement answers what, when, where and what to do, in that order, and fits on one screen. Replace the words in [brackets] and post.

Meetings and events

  • Notice: The monthly society meeting will be held on [day], [date] at [time] in the community hall. Agenda: [topics]. All members are requested to attend.

  • Reminder: Parent teacher meeting on [day], [date], from [time] to [time]. Please bring the report card.

  • Team lunch this Friday, [time], at [place]. Please reply YES or NO by Wednesday so we can book.

  • The Diwali get together will be on [date] at [time] on the terrace. Families are welcome. Please bring one dish to share.

Collections and contributions

  • We are collecting Rs [amount] per family for [purpose]. Please pay [Name] by [date], by UPI or cash, and send a message after paying.

  • Farewell gift for [Name]: we are collecting Rs [amount] each. Please send it to [Name] by [date]. The card is at the front desk for signing.

  • Accounts update: Rs [amount] collected, Rs [amount] spent on [items]. The balance of Rs [amount] is with the treasurer. Bills are available on request.

Water, power and repairs

  • Notice: Water supply will be shut from [time] to [time] on [date] for tank cleaning. Please store water in advance.

  • The lift in Block [block] will be under maintenance on [date] from [time] to [time]. Sorry for the trouble.

  • Power will be off from [time] to [time] tomorrow for line repair, as informed by the electricity office. Please plan accordingly.

Lost, found and help

  • Found: a set of keys near the main gate this morning. The owner may collect them from the security cabin.

  • Lost: a brown wallet near the parking area around [time]. If found, please message me. Thank you.

  • Does anyone know a reliable electrician who can come today? Please share the number. Thanks in advance.

  • A neighbour in [flat] needs a few units of [blood group] blood at [hospital]. Donors who can help, please call [number].

Nothing matches. Try a shorter word, or choose All.

Write for the person who reads only the first line

In a busy group, most members see the first line in the notification and nothing else. So the first line must hold the whole news: "Water supply off tomorrow, Tuesday, 10 am to 2 pm." Details can follow for those who open the message.

The order that works

  • What: the event or the problem, in the first line.
  • When: day, date and time together.
  • Where: the place, with a landmark or a location pin for outsiders.
  • What to do: reply, pay, bring, store water, move vehicles.
  • Whom to ask: one name and one number.

Make it easy to read

  • Keep it on one screen. Use short lines, with a blank line between parts.
  • A bold heading helps. In WhatsApp, a star on each side of a word makes it bold.
  • Do not write in all capitals. It reads as shouting.
  • For money collections, state the amount, the purpose, the last date, how to pay, and that a list of contributions will be shared. Then share it.

Before you post

Check the date against a calendar, since "Sunday the 14th" must be a Sunday. Check the phone number digit by digit. If the news is a warning or an appeal that came from elsewhere, confirm it with the source before passing it on: a false alarm in a society group spreads in minutes. For lost and found posts, do not put a child's full details or a home address in a large group. Give a contact number.

After you post

  • Pin the announcement if the group allows.
  • Post one reminder the day before, not five.
  • Close the loop: "Meeting held. Minutes attached." or "Found. Thank you all."

Make it yours

  • Put the date, day and time together: "Sunday, 14 [month], 10 am". People miss dates written far apart.
  • One announcement, one topic.
  • End with the action: "Reply YES by Friday" or "Pay at the office".
