Wedding anniversary invitation wording

An anniversary party is usually hosted by the children or by the couple themselves. The 25th (silver) and 50th (golden) anniversaries call for a fuller invitation. Type the names and the number of years to fill the cards.

Stays in your browser.

  • From the children

    [Years] years ago they said yes to each other. We are glad they did. Please join us to celebrate the wedding anniversary of our parents, [Couple]. [Date], [Time] [Venue] With love, [Host]

  • From the couple

    [Years] years, countless cups of tea and one long conversation. Please celebrate our anniversary with us. [Date], [Time] [Venue] [Couple]

  • Silver or golden jubilee

    With gratitude for [Years] years of togetherness, the family of [Couple] invites you to an evening of music, memories and dinner. Date: [Date] Time: [Time] Venue: [Venue]

  • Short, for WhatsApp

    [Couple] complete [Years] years of marriage! Small celebration on [Date], [Time], at [Venue]. Please come and bless them.

  • बच्चों की ओर से

    हमारे माता-पिता [Couple] के विवाह के [Years] वर्ष पूरे हो रहे हैं। इस खुशी के मौके पर आप सपरिवार आमंत्रित हैं। दिनांक: [Date] समय: [Time] स्थान: [Venue] निवेदक: [Host]

  • सरल संदेश

    साथ के [Years] साल! [Couple] की शादी की सालगिरह [Date] को मना रहे हैं। समय [Time], स्थान [Venue]. आप आएँगे तो खुशी और बढ़ जाएगी।

What to include

  • Names of the couple and the number of years.
  • Who is hosting.
  • Date, time and venue.
  • A note if it is a surprise for the couple.

Tips

  • Old photographs make a lovely display. Ask guests in the invitation to bring one if they have it.
  • For a surprise party, give a clear arrival time and one contact person.
  • For elderly couples, an early evening time and comfortable seating matter more than decoration.

Questions people ask

Which anniversaries have special names?

The 25th is widely called the silver jubilee and the 50th the golden jubilee. The 60th is often called the diamond jubilee.

Should gifts be mentioned?

For elders, a line such as "Only your blessings, please" is common and saves guests from guessing.
