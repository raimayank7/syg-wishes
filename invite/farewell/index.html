Farewell party invitation wording

Farewell parties say goodbye to a class, a colleague or a neighbour who is moving away. The best invitations are short and a little emotional without becoming sad. Choose a wording for school, college or office.

Stays in your browser.

  • School or college batch

    You taught us the shortcuts, the canteen menu and how to survive exams. Now it is our turn to say thank you. Farewell for [Name] [Date], [Time] [Venue] With love, [Host]

  • Office colleague

    Our colleague [Name] is moving on to a new chapter. Please join us for a small farewell. [Date], [Time] [Venue] Come with a memory to share.

  • Teacher's farewell

    A good teacher leaves a school, but never its students. Please join the farewell function for [Name]. Date: [Date] Time: [Time] Venue: [Venue] [Host]

  • Short notice

    Farewell party for [Name] on [Date] at [Time], [Venue]. Attendance of everyone is requested. Dress code will be shared in the group.

  • विदाई समारोह

    साथ बिताए दिन याद रहेंगे, और दोस्ती भी। [Name] के विदाई समारोह में आप सादर आमंत्रित हैं। दिनांक: [Date] समय: [Time] स्थान: [Venue] निवेदक: [Host]

  • सरल संदेश

    [Name] की विदाई पार्टी [Date] को [Time] बजे [Venue] में है। सबको आना है, और एक अच्छी याद साथ लानी है।

What to include

  • Who the farewell is for and who is hosting.
  • Date, time and place.
  • Dress code or theme, if any.
  • Any contribution, shared separately and politely, never as a demand in the invitation.

Tips

  • Check the date with the person or batch leaving before you announce it.
  • For school farewells, share the end time so parents can plan pick-up.
  • A shared photo album link after the party is a good follow-up message.

Questions people ask

What is a good title for a school farewell?

Simple titles work best: "Farewell 2027", "Goodbye, Batch of 2027" or the Hindi "Vidaai Samaroh".

How early should a farewell be announced?

One to two weeks is enough. Office farewells are often arranged within the last week of the person's notice period.
