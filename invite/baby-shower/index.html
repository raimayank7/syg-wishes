Baby shower and godh bharai invitation wording

A baby shower gathers the women of the family, and often now everyone, to bless the mother before the baby comes. Different regions have their own names for it: godh bharai, seemantham, dohale jevan, shaad. The wordings below can be used for any of them.

Stays in your browser.

  • Godh bharai, traditional

    With the blessings of our elders, [Host] invite you to the godh bharai ceremony of [Mother]. Date: [Date] Time: [Time] Venue: [Venue] Please come and bless the mother and the little one on the way.

  • Modern baby shower

    A little one is on the way, and we cannot keep calm! Join us for a baby shower for [Mother]. [Date], [Time] [Venue] Games, good food and lots of love.

  • From the parents

    We are counting days and tiny socks. Please join our baby shower and share our happiness. [Date] at [Time] [Venue]

  • Short, for WhatsApp

    You are invited to [Mother]'s baby shower on [Date], [Time], at [Venue]. Please come and shower her with blessings.

  • गोद भराई

    हमारे घर नन्हा मेहमान आने वाला है। इस खुशी में [Mother] की गोद भराई की रस्म रखी है। दिनांक: [Date] समय: [Time] स्थान: [Venue] आप आकर होने वाली माँ को आशीर्वाद दें। निवेदक: [Host]

  • छोटा संदेश

    [Mother] की गोद भराई [Date] को है, समय [Time], स्थान [Venue]. आप ज़रूर आइए, आपके आशीर्वाद के बिना रस्म अधूरी रहेगी।

What to include

  • The mother's name and who is hosting (her family, her in-laws or friends).
  • The name of the ceremony your family uses.
  • Date, time and venue.
  • Whether it is for women only or for everyone.

Tips

  • Hold the function when the mother is comfortable. Many families choose the seventh month, but follow your own custom and the doctor's advice.
  • Keep the programme short and give the mother a comfortable chair.
  • If friends are hosting as a surprise, check the date with a family member first.

Questions people ask

Do we mention whether it is a boy or a girl?

No. In India, finding out the sex of a baby before birth is against the law, so invitations simply celebrate the baby.

Who is invited to a godh bharai?

Traditionally the women of both families and close friends. Many families now invite men too. Say it clearly in the invitation so nobody is unsure.
