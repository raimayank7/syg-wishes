Birthday party invitation wording

A birthday invitation should make people smile before the party starts. Below are wordings for children, grown-ups and milestone years. Fill in the name, age and party details, then copy the one you like.

Stays in your browser.

  • Child's party

    [Name] is turning [Age]! Come for cake, games and lots of fun. [Date], [Time] [Venue] Please tell [Phone] if you can come.

  • From the child

    Hi! It is my birthday and I am going to be [Age]. Please come to my party on [Date] at [Time]. Place: [Venue] It will be more fun with you there. From, [Name]

  • Adult, relaxed

    Another year older, and I am calling it a reason for good food and good company. [Name]'s birthday dinner [Date], [Time] [Venue] Just bring yourself.

  • Milestone birthday

    [Age] years of [Name], and every one of them worth celebrating. Please join the family for a special evening. [Date], [Time] [Venue]

  • Surprise party

    Shh, it is a surprise! We are planning a surprise party for [Name]'s birthday. [Date], please reach by [Time] sharp. [Venue] Do not tell [Name]. Reply only to [Phone].

  • बच्चे का जन्मदिन

    हमारे प्यारे [Name] का जन्मदिन है! अब हो गए पूरे [Age] साल। केक, खेल और ढेर सारी मस्ती के लिए ज़रूर आइए। दिनांक: [Date] समय: [Time] स्थान: [Venue]

  • बड़ों का जन्मदिन

    [Name] के जन्मदिन की खुशी में एक छोटी सी दावत रखी है। [Date], [Time] [Venue] आप आएँगे तो खुशी दुगनी हो जाएगी।

What to include

  • Whose birthday it is, and the age if the person is happy to share it.
  • Date, start time and, for children, an end time so parents know when to pick up.
  • Full address or party hall name.
  • Who to reply to, and by when.
  • Anything special: a theme, swimming clothes, or whether brothers and sisters are welcome.

Tips

  • Two to three weeks of notice suits most birthday parties. For a class party, send it at least ten days before.
  • For small children, write whether parents should stay or drop the child.
  • Ask about food allergies in the invitation when you invite children you do not know well.
  • For a surprise party, write the arrival time in bold words and name one person for replies.

Questions people ask

Should I write "no gifts"?

Only if you mean it. A kind way is "Your presence is the present". Do not ask for money or a particular gift in the invitation.

How do I invite the whole class without leaving anyone out?

Either invite everyone or keep the list small and send the invitations privately to parents, never through the class group.
