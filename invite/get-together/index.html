Invitations

Get-together, kitty party and reunion invitation wording

Not every gathering needs a reason. These wordings are for a kitty party, a family lunch, a potluck with neighbours and a school or college reunion. Keep the tone light and the details exact.

Stays in your browser.

  • Kitty party

    Kitty time! This month the party is at my place. [Date], [Time] [Venue] Theme and game details will follow in the group. [Host]

  • Family lunch

    It has been too long since we all sat at one table. Family lunch on [Date], [Time] [Venue] Come hungry and bring the children. [Host]

  • Potluck

    One dish each, and a full table for all. Potluck dinner on [Date] at [Time] [Venue] Tell me what you are bringing so we do not end up with six kinds of rice.

  • Reunion

    Same faces, a few more grey hairs, and the same old jokes. Reunion of [Group] [Date], [Time] [Venue] Reply by this week so we can book the tables.

  • पारिवारिक मिलन

    बहुत दिन हो गए सबको एक साथ बैठे। [Date] को [Time] बजे हमारे घर खाने पर आइए। पता: [Venue] बच्चों को ज़रूर लाइए। [Host]

  • पुराने साथियों का मिलन

    वही दोस्त, वही किस्से, बस साल कुछ ज़्यादा हो गए। [Group] का मिलन समारोह [Date], [Time] [Venue] आने की सूचना इसी हफ्ते दे दीजिए।

What to include

  • The kind of gathering and who is hosting.
  • Date, time and place.
  • What to bring, wear or prepare: a dish, a theme colour, a game.
  • A reply date when you must book a place.

Tips

  • For a potluck, keep a shared list of dishes in the group, so the menu stays balanced.
  • For a reunion, pick the date by a quick poll first, then send the invitation.
  • For a kitty, send a reminder one day before with the theme.

Questions people ask

What is a kitty party?

It is a regular get-together, usually monthly, of a fixed group of friends. Members take turns to host. The name and the customs vary from group to group.

How do I split costs at a reunion without awkwardness?

Say the per-person amount clearly in a separate message and name one person who collects it. Keep money talk out of the invitation card itself.
