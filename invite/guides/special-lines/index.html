Special lines to add

Some things are awkward to say and still need saying: no gifts, limited seats, a party without children. One kind line, placed at the end of the invitation, does it. Tap Copy on any line below and paste it into your message or card.

No gifts, please

  • Your presence is the only present we need.
  • No gifts, please. Just bring your blessings.
  • Please, no boxed gifts. Your good wishes are enough for us.
  • कृपया कोई उपहार न लाएँ। आपका आशीर्वाद ही हमारा उपहार है।
  • केवल आशीर्वाद, कोई भेंट नहीं।

Only the named guests

  • We have reserved two seats in your honour.
  • Because of limited space, we are able to host only the guests named on this invitation. Thank you for understanding.
  • This is a small gathering of close family and friends, and we are so glad you are part of it.

Adults only

  • We love your little ones, but this evening is for grown-ups only.
  • To let all parents relax, this will be an adults-only celebration.

Children welcome

  • Children are most welcome. There will be a play corner for them.
  • Bring the whole family. The more children, the merrier.
  • बच्चों को ज़रूर साथ लाइए।

Dress code

  • Dress code: Indian festive.
  • Theme colour: shades of yellow.
  • Come comfortable. We will be sitting on the floor for the puja.
  • पहनावा: पारंपरिक।

Food and drink

  • Pure vegetarian dinner will be served.
  • Lunch follows the ceremony.
  • Please tell us about any food allergy when you reply.
  • High tea will be served at 5 pm.
  • कार्यक्रम के बाद भोजन का प्रबंध है।

Punctuality

  • The muhurat is at 10:15 am. Please be seated by 10.
  • The surprise starts at 7 sharp. Please arrive by 6:45.
  • कृपया समय का ध्यान रखें।

Parking and directions

  • Parking is available inside the venue.
  • The lane is narrow. Please park on the main road and walk in.
  • A location pin follows this message.

Unplugged ceremony

  • We request you to keep phones away during the pheras. Our photographer will share the pictures with everyone.
  • Please enjoy the moment with us and leave the photography to the team.

How to use these lines well

  • Use one or two special lines, not five. A list of rules makes an invitation feel like a notice.
  • Put them at the end, after the date, time and venue.
  • Say the difficult ones in person too. A line about "adults only" lands softer after a friendly phone call.
  • Never ask for money or a specific gift in the invitation.
