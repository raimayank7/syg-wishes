Engagement and roka invitation wording

An engagement is usually a smaller gathering than the wedding, so the invitation can be warmer and shorter. These wordings work for a ring ceremony, sagai or roka. Fill in the names and details once and copy the one that sounds like your family.

Stays in your browser.

  • Formal

    [Host] cordially invite you to the engagement ceremony of [Bride] and [Groom] on [Date] at [Time]. Venue: [Venue] Please come and bless the couple.

  • From the couple

    She said yes, he said yes, and the families said yes too! Join us as [Bride] and [Groom] exchange rings. [Date], [Time] [Venue]

  • Short, for WhatsApp

    Happy news! [Bride] and [Groom] are getting engaged on [Date] at [Venue], [Time] onwards. It is a small family function and we would love to have you with us.

  • Roka ceremony

    With great joy we invite you to the roka ceremony of [Bride] and [Groom]. Date: [Date] Time: [Time] Place: [Venue] Your blessings will be the first gift of their new journey. [Host]

  • सगाई का निमंत्रण

    बड़े हर्ष के साथ सूचित कर रहे हैं कि [Bride] और [Groom] की सगाई [Date] को होने जा रही है। समय: [Time] स्थान: [Venue] आप सपरिवार आकर दोनों को आशीर्वाद दें। निवेदक: [Host]

  • छोटा संदेश

    खुशखबरी! [Bride] और [Groom] की सगाई [Date] को है। समय [Time], स्थान [Venue]. घर का छोटा सा कार्यक्रम है, आपको आना ही है।

What to include

  • Names of the couple and the hosting family.
  • Whether it is a roka, a ring ceremony or a full engagement function, so guests know how to dress.
  • Date, time and venue. For a function at home, give the full address.
  • Lunch or dinner, if a meal follows the ceremony.

Tips

  • Two to three weeks of notice is comfortable for an engagement. For a roka at home, a week is common.
  • Keep the guest list of the engagement inside the guest list of the wedding. Someone invited to the engagement expects a wedding invitation too.
  • If the wedding date is fixed, you can add one line about it, so guests can keep the dates free.

Questions people ask

Is a roka the same as an engagement?

Not quite. A roka is the first formal step where both families agree to the match. A ring ceremony or sagai usually comes later and is a larger function. Some families hold both on one day.

Should the invitation mention gifts?

It is not needed. If you prefer no gifts, add a gentle line such as "Your blessings are the only gift we need".
