Invitation card maker

Type your details, pick a design and download a picture card to send on WhatsApp. Free, no login, no watermark. The card is made on your own device and nothing is uploaded.

The preview changes as you type.

Your text is remembered in this browser for next time.

How to make your card

  1. Choose the occasion under Start from. A heading and a starter message appear.
  2. Type the names, date, time and venue. Leave any box empty to hide that line.
  3. Tap a design. Try two or three: dark designs suit evening functions, light ones suit daytime.
  4. Choose the size, then tap Download card, or Share to send it straight to WhatsApp.

Tips for a card people can read

  • Keep the message to two or three lines. Long text becomes small text.
  • Write the weekday with the date, and am or pm with the time.
  • Send the address as text and a location pin as well. Nobody can tap an address inside a picture.
  • Check the card once on your own phone before sending it to everyone.

Choosing the design and size

  • Portrait fills the chat window and is the best choice for a card sent as a message.
  • Square suits a post or a group icon.
  • Story fills the phone screen for a status. Keep in mind that a status is seen by all your contacts.
  • Lettering: Serif and Classic look formal, for weddings and pujas. Plain is the easiest to read on a small screen and suits birthdays and get-togethers.
  • Photo: a clear, close photo of one or two faces works best in the circle. Use the original from your gallery, not a screenshot.

Before you send it to everyone

  • Read every name letter by letter, and check that the weekday matches the date.
  • Send the card to one family member first and ask them to read it on their phone.
  • Follow the card with the same details as text and a location pin, as explained in inviting on WhatsApp.
  • If you will also print cards, read ordering printed cards, and for the names on envelopes, how to address an invitation.

Need the words first? Pick an occasion on the home page, fill in your details and tap Make a card on the wording you like.

Questions people ask

Is my card or photo uploaded anywhere?

No. The card is drawn inside your browser on your own phone or computer. Your text and photo never leave your device.

Can I write in Hindi or Punjabi?

Yes. Type in any language your phone keyboard supports. The card uses the lettering installed on your device for that language.

Which size should I choose?

Portrait suits a WhatsApp message, Square suits a post, and Story fits a WhatsApp status or an Instagram story.

Can I print the card?

The picture is 1080 pixels wide, which is sharp on phones and fine for a small print such as a postcard. For a large printed card, give the wording to a printer.

Is there a watermark?

No. The card carries only what you type.
