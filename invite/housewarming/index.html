Housewarming and griha pravesh invitation wording

A new home feels like home once family and friends have walked in. These wordings cover both the traditional griha pravesh puja and a relaxed housewarming party. Add the new address carefully, because this is the first time most guests will look for it.

Stays in your browser.

  • Griha pravesh, formal

    With the grace of God, we are stepping into our new home. [Host] invite you to the griha pravesh puja on [Date] at [Time]. New address: [Venue] Please join us for the puja and stay for lunch.

  • Housewarming party

    New home, same old us! Come and see the place, and help us fill it with good memories. Housewarming on [Date], [Time] onwards [Venue] Call [Phone] if you cannot find the lane.

  • Short, for WhatsApp

    We have moved! Please join us for a small housewarming get-together on [Date] at [Time]. Address: [Venue]. Location pin follows this message.

  • With gratitude

    A home is built with bricks, and a little with the good wishes of people like you. Please bless our new home on [Date], [Time]. [Venue] [Host]

  • गृह प्रवेश

    ईश्वर की कृपा से हमारे नए घर का गृह प्रवेश [Date] को होने जा रहा है। पूजा का समय: [Time] पता: [Venue] आप सपरिवार आकर हमारे नए घर को अपने आशीर्वाद से पवित्र करें। पूजा के बाद भोजन का प्रबंध है। निवेदक: [Host]

  • सरल संदेश

    नया घर, नई शुरुआत! [Date] को गृह प्रवेश की पूजा रखी है, समय [Time]. पता: [Venue]. आप सबको आना है। रास्ता न मिले तो [Phone] पर फोन कर लीजिए।

What to include

  • The full new address with flat or house number, floor, society, and a nearby landmark.
  • Time of the puja and the time of the meal, if they differ.
  • A phone number for directions.
  • Parking information when the lane is narrow or the society has visitor rules.

Tips

  • Send the location pin right after the invitation message, so both stay together in the chat.
  • A griha pravesh puja often has a fixed muhurat. Ask guests to arrive fifteen minutes before it.
  • If the house is still being set up, say so with a smile. Nobody minds boxes at a housewarming.

Questions people ask

What is the difference between griha pravesh and a housewarming party?

Griha pravesh is the puja done when a family first enters a new home. A housewarming party is the social get-together. Many families do the puja with close family in the morning and call friends in the evening.

Is it fine to invite neighbours we have just met?

Yes. A housewarming is a friendly way to meet them. A short spoken invitation with the card is enough.
