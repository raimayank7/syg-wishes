How it works

  1. Choose the occasion. Each page has wording in different tones: formal, friendly, short for WhatsApp, and Hindi.
  2. Fill in your details once. Names, date, time and venue drop into every wording on the page.

Questions people ask

Is it free?

Yes. Every wording and the card maker are free, with no login and no watermark.

Can I change the wording?

Of course. Copy it, then edit it in WhatsApp or in the card maker until it sounds like you.

Do you have Hindi invitations?

Yes. Every occasion has Hindi wording in Devanagari, and a few have Punjabi wording in Gurmukhi. Use the language buttons on each page.

Are my names and details stored?

They stay in your own browser so the boxes are filled next time. They are not sent to us. Tap Clear to remove them.
